# Independent flatfile history and namespace: primary integration

Required-book control pages alone cannot prove consecutive baseline history or
discover physical economic files outside the retained-root traversal. This slice
adds independent bounded `baseline-history` and `physical-namespace` scopes.
History checks catalogue-required books, original roots, revision membership,
reservation coverage and terminal identity across a source-bound traversal.
Namespace checks captured physical families against independent native decoders,
including orphan files, unknown initialization and pending source inputs.

Exactly thirteen source/test paths from Plan5 peer
`4f277dcf806ecca667e141ac8628bb06cf0f8e71` are integrated. The baseline and
records headers were formatted before qualification; maintained imports are
byte-identical to the qualified candidate. The baseline-marker test supplies the
shared `audit_source_inputs()` helper used by the lifecycle suite and is included.
No shared producer, coordinator, schema, writer registry or activation change.
Existing central entries run the three complete original native suites; no
new reduced substitute suite or acceptance gate is introduced.

## Boundaries

This qualifies sanitizer-backed native codec/participant fixtures and independent
offline readers. No optional previous-qualifier/legacy-source arguments were
supplied, so this run makes no legacy-reader compatibility claim.
The new scopes may close their specifically named known initialized-book or
captured physical-namespace range. Whole sweep, native holdings, general orphan
closure, complete accounting and release flags remain false. Actual producer
and player journeys, populated upgrades, full service restore/fault recovery,
operational policy and mixed workloads remain open. Whole Plan5 and R1–R8 are
not complete; inactive behavior and the declined spell path remain preserved.
