All 11 records carry the duplicate-room warning. These are source-register classifications, not independently verified entry or return paths.

The four inbound and seven outbound figures in the coverage row are retained as source-register counts, but they must not be presented as a reliable Minauros entrance or exit until #51800 receives one unambiguous owner.
