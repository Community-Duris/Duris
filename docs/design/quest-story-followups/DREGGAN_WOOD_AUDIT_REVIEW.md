## Source identity and authored content

Local sources contain 188 rooms (#41001–#41189, with ten gaps), five mobile prototypes, and three objects. See the [area list](../../../areas/AREA#L196), [QST area index](../../../areas/qst/AREA#L180), [zone header](../../../areas/zon/dreggan_wood.zon#L1), and [coverage row 81](ZONE_COVERAGE_REGISTER.csv#L81).

This gives the area a clear haunted-forest and maze premise, but little distinct room-by-room history ([WLD](../../../areas/wld/dreggan_wood.wld#L1), [MOB](../../../areas/mob/dreggan_wood.mob#L1), [OBJ](../../../areas/obj/dreggan_wood.obj#L1), [ZON](../../../areas/zon/dreggan_wood.zon#L15)).
