# Dragonnia repository-wide source audit

Checkpoint 213 maps active `dragonnia` against its local WLD, MOB, OBJ, and ZON sources; active and unregistered WLD connections; typed consumers and producers; followed history; and compiled VNUM references. This is a static source audit. It does not inspect live server state, persisted reset values, player access, or gameplay. The [source dossier](../zone-stories/DRAGONNIA.md) summarizes the setting and builder questions.

## Source identity and authored content

The active area list calls the contributor `dragonnia` with note `*68`; its zone header is #68, titled “Dragonnia,” and its last room is #6869. The local WLD has 67 rooms, the MOB has 32 prototypes (#6801–#6899, with gaps), and the OBJ has 67 (#6800–#6866). The QST area list includes Dragonnia, but no local QST, SHP, story sidecar, or native Q/QA contract exists. See the [area list](../../../areas/AREA#L37), [zone header](../../../areas/zon/dragonnia.zon#L1), [QST area index](../../../areas/qst/AREA#L34), and [coverage row 79](ZONE_COVERAGE_REGISTER.csv#L79).

The room text sketches a dragon city with five color brotherhoods, initiation rooms, a palace, a golden dragon quarter, a Temple of Teviros and monastery, egg incubator, and a hazardous shaft. Rooms repeatedly describe the city as largely abandoned: roads once walked by thousands, dusty or unused halls, bones, corpses, and blood. The reset roster nevertheless loads guards, masters, clergy, dragons, an empress, a surviving golden dragon, and babies. The golden dragon is called the last of his kind, killed off by the empress’s forces; Dragonnia is described as a five-headed conqueror. The source gives no chronology to reconcile the ruins with the current reset population ([roads and halls](../../../areas/wld/dragonnia.wld#L97), [empress and golden dragon resets](../../../areas/zon/dragonnia.zon#L155), [mobile descriptions](../../../areas/mob/dragonnia.mob#L224)).

The area includes four fall fields: 80% at #6800 and 100% at #6860, #6861, and #6862. Descriptions warn of a slick shaft without refuge and say that the city base is too high to climb back up. The zone resets DemoDragon (#6801) in outside room #6803; its compiled handler blocks the `down` command in that room. Actual movement and fall outcomes are not tested here ([shaft](../../../areas/wld/dragonnia.wld#L1), [fall sequence](../../../areas/wld/dragonnia.wld#L1052), [reset](../../../areas/zon/dragonnia.zon#L108), [handler](../../../src/specs/specs.dragonnia.c#L310)).
