## Checkpoint 305: twenty direct builder questions and recommendations

1. **Which Plains actions should count as separate one-time story outcomes: orientation, accepted racewar lesson, sword receipt, and arrival?** Recommendation: keep each stage separate and award only on its own committed event.
2. **Should every character whose starting location is #22800 receive the tutorial tag?** Recommendation: preserve the explicit start hook and qualify creation, return, and restored-character paths before using tag absence as lesson proof.
3. **Should looking at the first sign remain an optional armor aid?** Recommendation: keep it utility-only unless the owner defines a separate lesson outcome; repeated spell effects must not create repeated story credit.
4. **Should the second sign’s actual LOOK/EXAMINE dispatch match its “Read Sign” prose?** Recommendation: either add the intended read alias or change the prose, then qualify the exact command without recording an invented mastery test.
5. **Should the paladin continue to accept visible-target CMD_ASK topics racewar and racewars while the tutorial tag is present?** Recommendation: retain both documented aliases and bind acceptance to the exact actor and lesson instance.
6. **What should happen when the accepted racewar topic is repeated after the tag is gone?** Recommendation: preserve the current peace response and ensure it grants neither a second sword nor a second lesson event.
7. **Can a missing tutorial tag prove that the paladin lesson was completed?** Recommendation: treat absence as ambiguous because starting state, migration, and staff changes can also remove or omit it.
8. **Should clearing the tag and granting sword #22804 remain one recoverable operation?** Recommendation: admit and commit the reward first, then publish the completion and dialogue from the confirmed result.
9. **Is the blessed sword the paladin’s scripted lesson reward or one of the seven sack-loaded items?** Recommendation: keep the scripted reward distinct from reset-loaded inventory and verify its committed recipient.
10. **Should the seven Heavens-owned objects continue to load inside sack #22807?** Recommendation: preserve their exact external prototypes and reset ownership; do not create local duplicates to make the sack appear complete.
11. **How should signs, stream, sack, and nested items remain available when active accounting suppresses item-producing resets?** Recommendation: qualify durable generation for required fixtures and their nested contents before the tutorial journey, preserving the authored reset order.
12. **Is the tutorial tag alone the intended gate to entering the stream?** Recommendation: preserve the current distinction between the lesson flag and sword possession unless the content owner explicitly changes the rule.
13. **Should stream arrival be recorded only after the move to Ailvio succeeds?** Recommendation: validate the destination and complete movement before changing tutorial resources or publishing success.
14. **Why does the stream reject real_room index zero with a greater-than-zero check?** Recommendation: use the established valid-room convention and qualify missing, valid-zero, and ordinary destinations.
15. **Should the stream refresh hit points and print success before destination confirmation?** Recommendation: publish both only after a confirmed move, with retry-safe behavior on failure.
16. **How should Ailvio archway #29236’s fixed type-25 target #22800 interact with its custom race/class-home procedure?** Recommendation: document the intended precedence and qualify the resulting destination and return before promising a round trip.
17. **Should the scripted stream’s direct destination remain Ailvio #29201 for every accepted tutorial?** Recommendation: keep the current cross-zone owner and verify the resolved destination and saved home state before counting departure.
18. **Is Grandma-of-Ako invitation #22808 intentionally retired or should it have a recoverable route?** Recommendation: keep it out of player guidance and story counts until the content owner chooses one complete source, recipient, and outcome.
19. **Is it intentional that newbie2 appears in QST AREA but has no local QST contracts or file?** Recommendation: preserve the current scripted tutorial and guidance-only sidecar without inventing native QST receipts unless an owner-authored contract is approved.
20. **Should the accepted lesson and travel be one-time even after reset, reconnect, or character restoration?** Recommendation: assign stable character, lesson, grant, and travel identities, and preserve legacy ambiguity instead of inferring completion from item possession or tag state.
