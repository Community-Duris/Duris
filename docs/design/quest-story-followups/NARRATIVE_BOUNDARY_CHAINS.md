# Narrative boundary chains

**Research record — proposed decisions only.** These cases are useful because
the native outcome appears intentionally narrower or different from the
dialogue's promise. The proposed journal boundary follows accepted mechanics
and leaves unresolved authorship choices explicit.

## QSF-THAR-01 — Chiln's medicine and the empty pouch

**Priority:** P2. **Confidence:** high that the final Chiln exchange is an
intentional swindle; whether the story was also meant to provide a separate
healing ending is not established. **Classification:** intentional narrative
twist; a healed-person ending is currently unimplemented.

### Exact route

Nebble accepts two named herbs and creates healing vial 132697. The text frames
the vial as help for a wounded halfling
([Nebble contract](../../../areas/qst/tharnadia.qst#L535)). Chiln then claims a
family pendant was stolen and asks for that vial plus pendant 132699. On
acceptance, the Q consumes both inputs, gives empty pouch 132698 and 3,000
experience, and retires Chiln ([Chiln contract](../../../areas/qst/tharnadia.qst#L364)).
The pouch's extra description identifies it as empty and says the player was
swindled
([pouch object](../../../areas/obj/tharnadia.obj#L3159)), while Chiln's own
response explicitly tells the player not to trust a thief. The empty result is
therefore evidence of the planned trick, not proof that the medicine cured
someone or that Chiln escaped a documented arrest.

The source promises assistance, but the hand-in ends with the trick. The
existing [Tharnadia dossier](../zone-stories/THARNADIA.md#L104) maps the route;
its [boundary notes](../zone-stories/THARNADIA.md#L190) likewise distinguish
the deceptive exchange from an implemented healing outcome.

### Proposed disposition

If journaled, call the endpoint “Chiln exposed the player to a swindle” or
equivalent and show the exact empty-pouch receipt. The herb collection/vial
preparation is optional context, not a successful healing event. Do not add
“medicine delivered,” “halfling healed,” “Chiln rescued,” or “thief captured”
credit to this Q.

If the builder intended a genuine healed-person branch, first name the positive
actor state and exact trigger that proves it, plus how it interacts with the
existing swindle. That would be a separate authored route, not a correction to
the empty pouch reward. Keep the exact current vial/pendant requirements,
consumption, pouch, experience, and retirement unchanged in any prose-only
repair.

**UNSHIPPED proposal — news wording if projection is later qualified:** “The
Tharnadia record now follows Chiln's accepted exchange through the empty pouch
and identifies the deception. Preparing the vial remains optional context; the
exchange does not claim that a wounded halfling was healed.”

## QSF-SUN-01 — Morning-sun ring and forest restoration

**Priority:** P2. **Confidence:** high for the exact reward and absence of a
restoration event in the reviewed local contract; builder intent for the
promised world change is unresolved. **Classification:** ambiguous authored
intent.

### Exact exchange

Oak spirit 82401 accepts sunbeam 82408 and returns 82409, the ring of the
morning sun ([native Q](../../../areas/qst/suntmpl.qst#L20)). The actual item is
a takeable finger-worn ring, not a mace ([object record](../../../areas/obj/suntmpl.obj#L148)).
The Q's dialogue promises a powerful mace and restoration of the woods, but
the reviewed exchange contains only an item retirement/reward. It does not set
a persistent forest condition. The [Temple of the Sun dossier](../zone-stories/TEMPLE_OF_THE_SUN.md#L9)
records this exact boundary and separates upstream demon access and the sunbeam
from the supplied proof accepted by the spirit.

### Proposed disposition

For the current mechanic, use the exact name/type and say the spirit exchanges
the sunbeam for the ring. Do not promise a mace, a changed forest, or successful
restoration. If the builder wants actual restoration, identify the world object
or actor whose state changes, the trigger, persistence/reset scope, and proof
event. Do not add a new area-wide world-state effect just to make the line
literal. Preserve sunbeam 82408, ring 82409, source access, and other recipes.

Keep finding/recovering a sunbeam, acquiring it by another valid route, and
turning it in as separate facts. The Q's item-only offer does not require
personal demon combat or a previous Trentloss exchange when the exact loose
sunbeam is supplied.

**UNSHIPPED proposal — news wording if projection is later qualified:** “The
Temple of the Sun record now names the ring actually returned for the sunbeam.
The current exchange does not claim a persistent forest restoration; its
ingredients, reward, and access remain unchanged.”
