## Checkpoint 380: Ten explicit builder questions and recommendations

The comprehensive Temple dossier records ten builder requirements. This checkpoint turns each into a direct question with a conservative recommendation grounded in the existing source; it does not choose an unapproved gameplay change.

1. **Spoken access (ZSQ-TEMPLE-SPOKEN-ACCESS):** What exact command and wording should open the sepulchre and throne routes, and should opening either route count as personal progress? The outer route spells `bdkn` in one direction and `bkdn` in the other; SAY clears `LOCKED`/`SECRET` but leaves `CLOSED`. **Recommendation:** publish the intended command sequence and spelling; preserve shared open routes and require an actor-specific successful operation only if personal credit is explicitly intended. Do not infer credit from an already-open exit.
2. **Master chest (ZSQ-TEMPLE-MASTER-CHEST-INTENT):** Should the chest’s current OPEN/LOCKED reset state remain, or should it start closed and locked? The ring is stocked in the chest, while the exchanged key can break on a successful unlock. **Recommendation:** preserve supplied ring access and current behavior until the builder confirms the intended initial state, relock/scarcity policy, and caption; make any source repair a separately named change.
3. **Locket subtree (ZSQ-TEMPLE-LOCKET-SUBTREE):** Which locket roots and nested contents are legitimate proof, and is the photo independently required or expected to survive the exchange? The native input checks the locket identity and consumes a selected root after capturing its child snapshot. **Recommendation:** do not add a photo requirement or promise its preservation; define root/child issuance, removal, reconnect, and replay rules before crediting nested contents.
4. **Hammer route (ZSQ-TEMPLE-HAMMER-ROUTE):** How should players reach the melting rooms and obtain or renew the stonecrusher? The ordinary room graph has no incoming walking edge to the melting rooms, but same-zone teleport and group-teleport code can select eligible destinations. **Recommendation:** specify the intended puzzle/travel and item-source lifecycle; retain current travel guards and do not call the room unreachable from WLD connectivity alone.
5. **Shared controls (ZSQ-TEMPLE-SHARED-CONTROLS):** Which PUSH, PULL, TOUCH, or STARE effects should count as an individual character’s progress when the control changes shared exit state? The latch’s target has asymmetric blocked directions, and the alternate drain return is part of the route. **Recommendation:** document exact commands and side effects, preserve the current shared routes, and record actor/target/exit/state evidence only for outcomes the builder explicitly wants credited.
6. **Clue intent (ZSQ-TEMPLE-CLUE-INTENT):** What walkthrough and exact clue text should resolve the bridge alphabet and the conflicting `bdkn`/`bkdn` password, and is Illyn’s “Fire is the key” line meant as atmosphere or an actionable clue? The well is a TRASH object, and basin prose does not bind a switch. **Recommendation:** confirm the intended solution before changing clues or captions; do not activate commented procedures or invent a new trigger, item, or objective.
7. **Narrative episodes (ZSQ-TEMPLE-NARRATIVE-EPISODES):** What concrete world-state changes, if any, should follow the Illyn, king, angel, and master outcomes? Current responses narrate peace, human form, reunion, and aid without creating replacement actors, escorts, released groups, or a permanent cure. **Recommendation:** describe each intended terminal state and its reset/recovery policy before granting rescue or cure credit; until then treat the current text as narrative outcomes only.
8. **Bundled proofs and rewards (ZSQ-TEMPLE-BUNDLES-REWARD-RECOVERY):** Should Sage’s four-item bundle be all-or-nothing, how should the angel’s two same-prototype rewards be distinguished, and what recovery is intended for interrupted hand-ins or wallet limits? The king and Ommsh also have valid outcomes with empty reward lists. **Recommendation:** preserve separate reward-source identities, do not multiply completion by possession or reward count, and define atomic consumption and recovery before changing journal credit.
9. **Daily sources (ZSQ-TEMPLE-SOURCE-DAILY-RENEWAL):** Which finite items or capped sources are meant to replenish daily, if any, and should reset availability promise a player a usable proof? Mode 1/cap 1 and the retiring recipients constrain current source availability; they do not guarantee a giver or fresh item. **Recommendation:** make no daily availability promise from static source matching; confirm runtime issuance and lifecycle before describing a guarantee, and retain the configured caps and item-reset refusal meanwhile.
10. **Foreign ownership (ZSQ-TEMPLE-FOREIGN-OWNERSHIP):** Can one dagger #18309 ever satisfy both a Temple objective and Hall’s separate Q223 child recipe, and should imported epic node #358 or unused Temple actors create any local outcome? Hall consumes the dagger for its own letter exchange; #358 is separately guarded, and the unplaced statue/reward-only prototypes are not extra objectives. **Recommendation:** keep the Hall recipe and its journal foreign, treat one physical dagger as one consumable proof, and preserve current ownership boundaries unless the builder defines an explicit shared-copy policy.

These ten answers remain builder decisions. Source, connectivity, typed-reference, title/history, and DO NOT MOVE findings remain unchanged; no native game behavior, route, reward, reset, or quest accounting was modified.
