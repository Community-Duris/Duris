# `torr` builder, manifest, and loader questions

The unlisted “City of Fehlnan” has a 300-room map and quest text that closely
parallels active City of Torrhan, but the source history does not establish a
replacement link. These questions preserve that uncertainty and the source
selection caveats. See the [Torr audit](TORR_AUDIT_REVIEW.md) and the
[City of Torrhan dossier](../zone-stories/CITY_OF_TORRHAN.md).

1. **Source ownership:** Who owns the six `torr` WLD/MOB/OBJ/ZON/QST/SHP files? ([inventory](TORR_AUDIT_REVIEW.md)) **Recommendation:** keep all six paths in place until their source and tooling owners are known.
2. **Place identity:** Is “City of Fehlnan” an earlier name, a separate city, or a title left over from another build? ([old title](../../../areas/zon/torr.zon#L2), [selected Torrhan title](../../../areas/zon/torrhan.zon#L2)) **Recommendation:** preserve the authored ZON title and ask its builder before changing names or manifests.
3. **Torrhan relationship:** Does the shared King Torrhan, declining market, dragon-scale clue, hidden beach entrance, Rhithel, and Zrilxa story mean this map is an earlier version of active Torrhan? ([old QST](../../../areas/qst/torr.qst#L1), [active QST](../../../areas/qst/torrhan.qst#L28)) **Recommendation:** treat the story overlap as evidence to resolve, not as replacement authorization.
4. **Historical provenance:** Is there a builder record before the repository's 2009 directory-move history that explains how both maps came to coexist? ([tracked-history review](TORR_AUDIT_REVIEW.md)) **Recommendation:** retain distinct VNUM identities until that provenance is found or the owner confirms none exists.
5. **World-list omission:** Why is `torr` absent from `areas/AREA` while `torrhan` is selected? ([world list](../../../areas/AREA#L251)) **Recommendation:** ask the world-list owner before activating, retiring, or removing any source family.
6. **Parallel market rooms:** Are old #79000 and active #66600 intentionally two versions of the same market square? ([Fehlnan](../../../areas/wld/torr.wld#L1), [Torrhan](../../../areas/wld/torrhan.wld#L1)) **Recommendation:** compare their room ownership and intended text revisions with the map builder; do not merge by description similarity.
7. **Quest-file ownership:** Should `torr.qst` remain a source-only copy of the Torrhan story, or is there an external consumer? ([QST list](../../../areas/qst/AREA#L1), [legacy QST](../../../areas/qst/torr.qst#L1)) **Recommendation:** preserve the QST file until its author and quest-manifest owner identify its canonical use.
8. **Character-name variants:** Which spelling and identity are intended for Merthona/Marthona in the legacy QST compared with current Marthona? ([legacy names](../../../areas/qst/torr.qst#L4), [active names](../../../areas/qst/torrhan.qst#L28)) **Recommendation:** retain the original text until the story owner resolves the variant.
9. **Legacy QST contracts:** Are all 22 Q/QA contracts and ten giver headers still useful as source evidence? ([legacy QST](../../../areas/qst/torr.qst#L1), [coverage row](ATTIC_CANDIDATE_DEPENDENCY_REGISTER.csv#L107)) **Recommendation:** keep the full QST intact even though its path is absent from `qst/AREA`.
10. **Generated QST:** Has the deployed `areas/world.qst` been built from the current `qst/AREA`, and what process owns that artifact? ([manifest](../../../areas/qst/AREA#L1), [builder](../../../areas/src/qst/make_qst.c#L54), [runtime input](../../../src/world/quest.c#L79)) **Recommendation:** verify the build and deployment inputs before making any QST-path decision.
11. **Parallel quest arc:** Should the legacy owl-lady, princess, hypnotized-king, and secret-palace sequence remain represented only by active Torrhan's quest source? ([legacy princess arc](../../../areas/qst/torr.qst#L200), [active princess arc](../../../areas/qst/torrhan.qst#L246)) **Recommendation:** have both quest owners reconcile the two versions before copying or deleting contract text.
12. **Portal placement:** Is object #79046's portal to local room #79180, placed in #79205, the intended entrance for the legacy city? ([object](../../../areas/obj/torr.obj#L681), [placement](../../../areas/zon/torr.zon#L412), [destination](../../../areas/wld/torr.wld#L3659)) **Recommendation:** preserve its object and reset records together until the map owner confirms its role.
13. **Tunnel falls:** Are the fall chances on #79151–#79153 (10, 5, and 3) intentional for the underground passage? ([directives](../../../areas/wld/torr.wld#L3113), [L3132](../../../areas/wld/torr.wld#L3132), [L3151](../../../areas/wld/torr.wld#L3151)) **Recommendation:** keep the directives and their south exits paired pending a source-map review.
14. **Directed reach:** Are rooms #79264–#79270 intended to remain outside the raw directed walk from #79000? ([room #79264](../../../areas/wld/torr.wld#L5207), [room #79270](../../../areas/wld/torr.wld#L5309), [graph review](TORR_AUDIT_REVIEW.md)) **Recommendation:** review their one-way source exits with the mapper before changing directions or exits.
15. **Chasm approach:** Should #79092 south to active Mist Chasm #15052 remain as a source-only boundary? ([source edge](../../../areas/wld/torr.wld#L1909), [Chasm review](MIST_CHASM_AUDIT_REVIEW.md#inactive-approaches-and-attic-dependencies)) **Recommendation:** preserve the original direction and VNUM; keep it separate from current ingress until `torr` activation is authorized.
16. **Reset ownership:** Are the 517 local reset commands preserved for the old map only? ([ZON reset set](../../../areas/zon/torr.zon#L409)) **Recommendation:** retain the ZON as a separate reset set and do not import it into active Torrhan.
17. **Shop records:** Are the six shops, their 14 local products, and keeper/room reset pairs still intended source data? ([early shops](../../../areas/shp/torr.shp#L1), [later shops](../../../areas/shp/torr.shp#L108), [audit](TORR_AUDIT_REVIEW.md)) **Recommendation:** keep the SHP records with their matching MOB, OBJ, and ZON identities.
18. **Shop loader path:** Which deployment path is authoritative: the manifest-built `areas/world.shp` or the source glob fallback that reads every `areas/shp/*.shp` when the aggregate is absent? ([world build](../../../Makefile#L137), [builder](../../../areas/src/shp/make_shp.c#L26), [aggregate path](../../../src/world/db.h#L22), [fallback](../../../src/economy/shop.c#L3278)) **Recommendation:** have the runtime/build owner confirm the path before labeling these shops unavailable or removing them.
19. **Map/editor and story assets:** Is there an external map export or story sidecar for `torr` despite no checked-in `areas/map/torr.json` or `areas/story/torr.story.json`? ([audit inventory](TORR_AUDIT_REVIEW.md)) **Recommendation:** ask the editor and story owners before moving or archiving any source.
20. **Persisted and shared references:** Are any deployed quest states, admin tools, editor projects, or external scripts keyed to the old `torr` paths or VNUMs? ([current identity question](../zone-stories/CITY_OF_TORRHAN.md#explicit-builder-questions-and-recommended-resolutions), [audit](TORR_AUDIT_REVIEW.md)) **Recommendation:** require a separate owner inventory before any migration or archive proposal.
