# Yggdrasil Guild Hall Audit Review

Reviewed October 11, 2026, Checkpoint 464. This is a static source, story, WLD graph, followed-history, shop, and typed-reference review of the unregistered `circlegh` component. It does not establish player access, reset execution, shop operation, or permission to move or activate these files.

## Source scope and title

The current source family contains [WLD](../../../areas/wld/circlegh.wld), [MOB](../../../areas/mob/circlegh.mob), [OBJ](../../../areas/obj/circlegh.obj), [ZON](../../../areas/zon/circlegh.zon), and [SHP](../../../areas/shp/circlegh.shp). The ZON header is #9, titled “Yggdrasil Guild Hall,” with last VNUM #45417 and reset mode 0. The WLD defines 16 rooms: #45400–#45412 and #45415–#45417. The MOB defines 13 prototypes (#45400–#45404, #45410–#45413, and #45415–#45418); the OBJ defines four prototypes (#45410, #45411, #45415, and #45416); and the SHP has one shop record #45400. There is no same-stem QST or separate story sidecar. The component is absent from the full [area manifest](../../../areas/AREA) and [quest manifest](../../../areas/qst/AREA); the raw area-note field in the coverage row is blank.

The ZON last-VNUM field #45417 is one below mobile #45418, which has a reset in room #45417. The raw zone value 9 also differs from the #454 VNUM/100 band used by the rooms. Preserve these source values until the editor and area owners confirm their conventions.

## Tree, roots, gods, and creatures

The first connected map section begins at “A Quiet Camp Surrounded by Roots.” It rises through the base and lower branches of a giant ash, a leaf-built room, branches dedicated to faith, an arms-and-armor room, a branch leading into a sparkling plane, a giant squirrel’s nest, and the crest of Yggdrasil ([camp](../../../areas/wld/circlegh.wld#L1), [faith branch](../../../areas/wld/circlegh.wld#L102), [armory](../../../areas/wld/circlegh.wld#L115), [sparkling-plane branch](../../../areas/wld/circlegh.wld#L129), [squirrel’s nest](../../../areas/wld/circlegh.wld#L143), [crest](../../../areas/wld/circlegh.wld#L156)). Its room prose names the three Norns—Urd, Skuld, and Verdandi—and their weaving of a new hero’s destiny. Heidrun is a goat seeking something to graze on ([mobile prototypes](../../../areas/mob/circlegh.mob#L14)).

The second connected section is the root entrance, elevated tree room, and root tunnel also copied into `circle_op`. Its four light/tree golems recur there. The third section describes a warm hollow at the base of a fir, a high fir top exposed to cold and snow, and a root entrance rising through the trunk. Four reset prototypes guard that section: a snow wolf, snowy owl, pine tree, and mossy boulder ([root room](../../../areas/wld/circlegh.wld#L222), [fir top](../../../areas/wld/circlegh.wld#L237), [root entrance](../../../areas/wld/circlegh.wld#L257), [mobiles](../../../areas/mob/circlegh.mob#L135)).

The ZON has seven external O imports from Player Castles: object #11052 in rooms #45400, #45410, and #45417; #11013 in room #45409; and #11102 in rooms #45409, #45411, and #45416 ([reset list](../../../areas/zon/circlegh.zon#L9), [Player Castles definitions](../../../areas/obj/player_castles.obj#L137)). Preserve those exact source references; they are not active `circlegh` resets.

Four local O commands place the candidate’s portal objects: #45416 and #45411 in room #45407, #45410 in #45412, and #45415 in #45415. Their stored destinations are #45407 for objects #45410/#45415, #45412 for #45411, and #45415 for #45416 ([objects](../../../areas/obj/circlegh.obj#L1)). Two descriptions raise identity questions: object #45411 says it transports to “Goodie Cont” but targets local root tunnel #45412; object #45416 says “Ice Crag” but targets local room #45415, “A Huge Root at the Base of the Tree.” The reset placements and destinations are preserved here as source evidence, not as confirmed routes.

The ZON contains 13 M commands, one for each mobile prototype. The Norns and Heidrun are placed in #45400; squirrel #45400 is placed in the giant squirrel’s nest, #45408; the four shared golems #45410–#45413 are placed in #45410; and the snow wolf, snowy owl, pine tree, and boulder #45415–#45418 are placed in #45417. Ten G commands give the squirrel the same ten product VNUMs listed in the SHP.

The SHP defines one non-roaming shop with keeper #45400 and room #45400, but the ZON places its squirrel keeper in #45408. The product VNUMs are #364, #377, #398, #3050, #412, #347, #108, #109, #111, and #53. The full manifest includes Heavens, which defines nine of those products including #53, and Verz1, which defines #3050 ([Heavens](../../../areas/obj/heavens.obj#L3796), [Verz1](../../../areas/obj/verz1.obj#L850), [Heavens manifest entry](../../../areas/AREA#L2), [Verz1 manifest entry](../../../areas/AREA#L15)).

The checked-in `areas/world.shp` is absent. In non-mini mode, [`open_shop_stream`](../../../src/economy/shop.c#L3267) falls back to concatenating `areas/shp/*.shp`, so that fallback can include `circlegh.shp` independently of full WLD membership. The loader maps produced object VNUMs through [`real_object`](../../../src/economy/shop.c#L3374) and keeper #45400 through [`real_mobile`](../../../src/economy/shop.c#L3493); shop use resolves configured room #45400 through [`real_room`](../../../src/economy/shop.c#L3092). Neither the keeper mobile nor the shop room has an active full-list definition. These paths expose a static identity and placement mismatch; they do not establish that the shop is operational.

## Followed history, identity, and retention

Followed WLD/MOB/OBJ/ZON/SHP history stays on the `circlegh` stem from the 2009 area-tree move through later same-stem maintenance. The 2026 ANSI cleanup also touched `circlegh.obj`; no tracked rename or explicit replacement was found. The separate `circle_op` source shares exact records but has its own title and path. Other unregistered Yggdrasil guildhall sources `rf_gh` (#28900–#28912) and `ygg` (#30400–#30412) occupy different bands and do not establish a rename or common room identity.

Keep `circlegh` RETAIN IN PLACE. Before any separate path or activation proposal, resolve the three isolated map components, zone/range values and #45418 bound, the negative and two outbound exits, portal descriptions and destinations, seven Player Castles imports, squirrel/shop keeper location, stock and fallback behavior, shared `circle_op` blocks, dynamic routes, editor/tooling, and persisted room/mobile/object ownership.
