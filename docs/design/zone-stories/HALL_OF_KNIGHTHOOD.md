The active `knight` contributor is zone 709. Its source-comprehensive review is complete for this checkpoint. The four listed native files define a combat test and equipment rooms; there is no local QST, SHP, or `knight.story.json`. This review adds no campaign endpoint or gameplay change. Keep the contributor **DO NOT MOVE**.

## The authored test and its map

The entrance at #70900 bears a cross-and-sword symbol and warns that a visitor is committed after entering. Room #70901's sign calls the first section the Test of Strength and says magic is not allowed in the hallways. The colored sequence runs from White Knight #70902 through Green #70903, Blue #70904, Yellow #70905, Red #70906, and Crimson #70907, then reaches the Hall #70908. The signs name Test of Speed at #70908, Test of Truth at #70914, Test of Resilience at #70918, and warn against entering Convergence from #70923. The Truth sign says evil and neutral visitors will not be tolerated, but the reviewed WLD/reset sources do not encode an alignment gate. The prose describes flaming arrows, airborne morningstars, and moving whips in the colored rooms; source room/reset data does not establish those as working hazards.

The 41 WLD records contain 80 resolving exits: 79 local and one outward. The local weak graph has a 40-room component and the isolated world-list bookend #70999. With every authored edge treated as available, #70900 reaches 36 rooms, while 39 rooms can reach back to #70900. Rooms #70936–#70939 and #70999 are outside forward reach from #70900; #70937 has no exits. The same source census records no falls or currents. These graph counts do not apply closed/locked reset state or prove player, mobile, spell, or portal behavior.

One active Surface boundary is reciprocal: #70900 south to Surface #639011 and Surface #639011 north back to #70900 ([outbound record](../quest-story-followups/WORLD_CROSS_AREA_EXIT_REGISTER.csv#L848), [Surface return](../quest-story-followups/WORLD_CROSS_AREA_EXIT_REGISTER.csv#L1671)). A second authored approach is only in unregistered `surf.wld`: room #259810 north to #70900 ([source exit](../../../areas/wld/surf.wld#L1255034), [target line](../../../areas/wld/surf.wld#L1255041)). That edge is source-only, not active ingress.

The [reset file](../../../areas/zon/knight.zon#L1) starts with 38 door resets. It closes the outside entrance and the return from #70901, then locks several colored test pairs with keys carried by the corresponding knights. Later resets close the Pirate's Tunnel, Holy Auditorium, class-room, and Convergence passages. The west exit from Convergence #70924 carries key VNUM #70917 in the WLD, but that WLD value marks a pickable door rather than a locked state; its reset state 1 closes the door and clears the locked bit in the checked reset code ([exit](../../../areas/wld/knight.wld#L689), [reset](../../../areas/zon/knight.zon#L152), [state handling](../../../src/world/db.c#L7878)). The checked source therefore does not make #70917 a current gate key; live reset state was not played. The halls #70908, #70914, and #70918 are described as rests. At #70908 the prose mentions a westward magic portal, but that room has no west WLD exit, local portal object, or local type-25 destination. Convergence #70924 describes one black door as its only exit, while its WLD records exits east to #70923 and west to #70925. Staff of Life #70925 describes a shimmering west door and has a west exit directly to #70900, alongside its east return to #70924.

The #70923 prose says a small fountain begins running and mushrooms suddenly appear; its local reset places the small spout object #70969, but no mushroom object reset is present. The Pirate's Tunnel and late-room records need separate qualification from the main test. The tunnel branches through #70909–#70935. Room #70936 returns south to #70914 but is not reachable from the entrance. Lobos loads at #70939; its down exit leads through #70938 into #70909, while its other five exits lead into trap room #70937, which has no exits. The entrance cannot reach #70937–#70939 through authored WLD edges. Room #70999 is expressly a no-exit bookend for the world command and has no ordinary route.

The 105 mobile reset commands include the colored knights and squires, Pirate's Tunnel mobs, class leaders at #70924, Kithron at #70925, and Lobos at #70939. The two follower commands and reset mode 1 are source data, not a played population or replenishment guarantee.

Its active compiled object procedure responds when a living character wearing it says `path`, subject to an 800-second timer, and invokes `spell_pass_without_trace`; this is an item callback, not a static room link ([assignment](../../../src/specs/specs.assign.c#L1402), [procedure](../../../src/specs/specs.knight.c#L18)). The compiled Lobos-coat assignment for #70924 is commented out ([assignment history](../../../src/specs/specs.assign.c#L1735)); the local reset still equips the coat on Lobos.
- The Hall's resets import White Haven objects #55269, #55325, and #55379 for Kithron, Saint Verdonnaly, and Arthel ([Kithron reset](../../../areas/zon/knight.zon#L372), [Saint Verdonnaly reset](../../../areas/zon/knight.zon#L303), [Arthel reset](../../../areas/zon/knight.zon#L333)). The imports keep their White Haven prototypes and any future movement decision must preserve those typed references.
- The reset imports Heavens objects #72 and #360 to the Staff of Life. Heavens currently defines #72 as a magical fountain and #360 as a rune-covered monolith ([#72](../../../areas/obj/heavens.obj#L1000), [#360](../../../areas/obj/heavens.obj#L3741)). No outside ZON reset, SHP product, duplicate external mobile/object definition, or external type-25 destination was found for VNUMs #70900–#70999. Outside QST/story input references are limited to #70970 and #70976 above. There is no local story sidecar or quest command to supply a native accepted completion, and these external item receipts do not establish a Hall quest.
