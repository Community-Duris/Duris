# The Fog Enshrouded Wood

Active `foggy_woods` is zone #900, titled “The Fog Enshrouded Wood,” with 213 rooms (#90000–#90220), 32 mobile prototypes, and 33 object prototypes. It is listed in both `areas/AREA` and `areas/qst/AREA`, but has no local QST, shop file, or story sidecar. Its 136 non-`S` zone commands include 76 mobile placements and 20 door-state commands. See the [area list](../../../areas/AREA#L318), [zone header and resets](../../../areas/zon/foggy_woods.zon#L1), [QST area index](../../../areas/qst/AREA#L283), [rooms](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L1), [mobile prototypes](../../../areas/mob/foggy_woods.mob#L1), [object prototypes](../../../areas/obj/foggy_woods.obj#L1), and [coverage row 100](../quest-story-followups/ZONE_COVERAGE_REGISTER.csv#L100).

## A logging camp swallowed by the forest

The northern approach opens into a cold, animal-haunted wood. Two exits from room #90000 lead into Surface rooms. The trail then runs south through an abandoned logging camp whose rooms describe smashed wagons and barracks, slashed bunks, human remains, and a chained, mangled dog. A newly disturbed grave and a wolf clawing at it add another unexplained death. The source never says what attacked the camp or why its logging crew vanished. See the [forest approach](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L1), [camp entrance](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L238), [grave](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L1051), and [chained dog](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L825).

The general store (#90013) still describes goods for sale. A reset-closed secret stair drops to a basement where Ulon (#90022) is reset; his mobile description calls him a shopkeeper and says he was spared whatever killed the camp. The local source has no .shp file to define his stock. A second secret passage under a fallen tree leads to a burrow, while the rest of the wood moves through streams, warm springs, tree hollows, cliffs, a log wall, and a misty perimeter. See the [store](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L310), [hidden stair and basement](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L749), [Ulon](../../../areas/mob/foggy_woods.mob#L329), and [burrow entrance](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L1980).

## Ashen fog and giant country

The forest grows denser and more dangerous north of the camp. Fog that smells foul and makes breathing difficult gives way to giant tracks, a windfall clearing, and a log palisade broken by a fallen tree. Room #90161 is an occupied trapper’s hut: its description names steel traps, oil, and supplies, and the reset places a grizzled trapper there. Nearby paths lead toward the fog giants’ caves. See the [ashen fog](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L3183), [giant tracks](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L3861), [trapper’s hut](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L4293), and [trapper mobile](../../../areas/mob/foggy_woods.mob#L197).

The cave entrance opens into giant living spaces: a large cavern with pallets and cooking pots, a lavish shaman’s cave, storage caverns, and a huge fog-filled cavern above a warm underground pond. A high opening at the pond points toward the snowy mountains; a reciprocal exit leads to Underworld3. Rat tunnels below the cavern are explicitly too small for giants. They continue to the Pathway of Spears, where ash spears, shriveled heads, dark stones, and rune-like marks lead to an incense-scented grove. The zone resets the Beastmaster and a spirit-wolf guardian in that grove. The Beastmaster’s rune book and ashen spear make him the area’s clearest link to its rune magic; the source does not define a quest or event sequence around him. See the [cave entrance](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L5281), [giant cavern](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L5303), [shaman’s cave](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L5365), [underground pond](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L5552), [rat tunnels](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L5597), [Pathway of Spears](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L5674), [Beastmaster](../../../areas/mob/foggy_woods.mob#L78), and [rune objects](../../../areas/obj/foggy_woods.obj#L1).

## Keys, hidden doors, and authored item links

The logging-camp store and basement, the fallen-tree burrow, the trapper’s hut, and the cave passage each have reset-set door states. In the caves, the south door from #90207 to #90208 starts closed, locked, secret, and blocked. The zone places the huge wooden key (#90024) on the fog giant shaman in #90205, and a gigantic torch switch (#90010) in #90210 is configured to clear the blocked flag. That switch does not itself clear the door’s closed, locked, or secret flags. See the [door resets](../../../areas/zon/foggy_woods.zon#L8), [passage](../../../areas/wld/foggy_woods.wld#L5412), [shaman](../../../areas/mob/foggy_woods.mob#L213), [key](../../../areas/obj/foggy_woods.obj#L260), and [switch placement](../../../areas/zon/foggy_woods.zon#L109).

In the underwater pond room, a loose brass key (#90023) matches the lock field on the mineral chest (#90022); gold coins (#90026) are nested inside. Two objects also have clear outside-area uses: Icecrag’s quest asks for two bottles of local wine (#90017), and Alatorin’s armor service grants the local silver-studded white dragon scalemail (#90018). Verspin resets the local gold-coin object (#90026) into a chest. These exact source links are recorded in the [repository-wide audit](../quest-story-followups/FOGGY_WOODS_AUDIT_REVIEW.md#typed-dependencies-and-source-only-references). See the [pond objects](../../../areas/zon/foggy_woods.zon#L110), [key and chest prototypes](../../../areas/obj/foggy_woods.obj#L244), [Icecrag exchange](../../../areas/qst/icecrag.qst#L320), [Alatorin service](../../../areas/qst/alatorin.qst#L5724), and [Verspin reset](../../../areas/zon/verspin.zon#L228).

The water-logged scroll object #90025 contains a fragmentary note about Northland runes and their use by shamans, but no local reset places it. It is the only local object prototype unused by the zone’s reset commands. See the [scroll](../../../areas/obj/foggy_woods.obj#L268).
