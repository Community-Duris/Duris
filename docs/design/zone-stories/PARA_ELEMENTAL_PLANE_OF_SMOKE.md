## Planar travel and hazards

There is no ordinary foreign boundary. Older Air object 24407 is O-loaded at
24405 and enters fresh-air pocket 139832; older Fire object 25406 is O-loaded
at 25464 and enters fire pocket 139894. Both use ITEM_TELEPORT destination/
ENTER/unlimited values. Local blue 139821 returns to 24405 from 139832 and
139944; red 139820 returns to 25464 from 139894 and 139944. These older planes
are distinct from the later Air/Fire areas; prototype destination is evidence
of a declared route, not played perception or surviving accessibility.

The 125-room grid 139801–139925 has six-direction routes across five layers.
Its Air vortex starts west of 139851; Fire vortex starts south of 139875.
Disc 139802 at the lair 139863 enters sphere 139937, whose disc 139807 returns.
Rijak's four sphere rooms have ordinary internal routes. Portal 139819 from
139940 enters antechamber 139941 one way; its south route returns to grid
139838. Forge 139945 descends to exodus 139944, with both return portals and
no ordinary upward return. Spawning 139935 connects through 139933 to the lair;
holding rooms 139934/139936 have no exits. Rare room 139943 has outward routes
but no reviewed ordinary incoming exit; vault occupants actually start at 139942.

Actual room sectors are Fire Plane 131, Air Plane eighteen, inside three and
Negative Plane one. [Arrival handling](../../../src/world/handler.c#L1426)
schedules shared terrain effects: most fiery rooms are actual heat hazards;
Air Plane requires appropriate flight for movement. The fresh-air pocket has
NO_MOB but no SAFE flag. Most physical rooms block sight; principal smoke rooms
are magically dark. Descriptive air, flight, fire resistance or sanctuary claims
must not be promoted to accepted access or guaranteed safety.

**Forge terrain remains a pending repair decision.** Smouldering Forge 139945
is sector 35, [SECT_NEG_PLANE](../../../src/core/defines.h#L493), and actual
[arrival handling](../../../src/world/handler.c#L1435) schedules negative-plane
life-force drain. Its fire/heat imagery supplies a concrete intent mismatch,
but selecting a replacement sector changes gameplay and needs builder judgment.
Forge object 139830 is an ordinary zero-capacity container with no operating
special; submitting a crafting bundle to Erk is the actual service endpoint.
