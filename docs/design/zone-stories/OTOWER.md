# The Outcasts Tower

Active source stem **otower**, zone 113, contains 51 rooms (#11300–#11350), 34 mobile prototypes, and 23 object prototypes. It is listed in the full world and QST area manifests. The source set has WLD, MOB, OBJ, and ZON files, but no local QST, SHP, or story sidecar. The tower has a dark mountain approach, a mixed-race army of Lolth worshippers and necromancers, a hidden upper sanctum, and a flooded cave route described as an emergency exit.

## Story in the source

The approach begins in burnt, dark terrain beneath a mountain range. The tower gates are battered black steel. A narrow crack between the tower and cliff offers another route into the mountains; beyond it, the text describes steep trails, loose rock, a rockslide, and an alpine grove. The grove is presented as the only sanctuary in the range. Aleilinal, an elven huntress, has made it a refuge for creatures and travelers opposed to the tower. A hidden cave nearby is occupied by a hungry stone giant.

Inside, the tower is a disciplined stronghold rather than a simple monster lair. Its first floor contains guards, training and punishment rooms, and a warbeast fed with failed trainees. The second floor separates spell study, necromancy, and worship: Trankula teaches corpse raising in a room of preserved bodies; the drow priestess Lerinsacia leads sacrifices to Lolth; and a duergar shrine instead serves the sick and dying. Ogres, trolls, drow, duergar, kuo-toa, goblins, orcs, humans, and other initiates are described as parts of the same force.

The top level holds the tower's named leaders. Vinxinor commands goblin and orc hordes. Arcanusinil is a drow sorcerer and brother to Arcanusin, said to be away on a mission in the ice forest or tundra. Grolanthantor is a battle-scarred troll mercenary. Ximixix is a psionic lich, possibly an illithid or drow, whose influence organizes the tower's otherwise disparate followers. His shadow dragon is described as either befriended or controlled. The floor is made of bone, and the room descriptions frame it as a later addition to the conquered tower.

Behind the tower, a shadowed swamp collects fallen travelers, clothing, rope, and corpses from the dangerous mountain trails. Alligator families feed on fish, goats, people thrown out of the tower, and adventurers. A deep pool descends beneath the tower to a kuo-toan cave. The cave prose calls this route the tower's emergency exit; an active reciprocal WLD boundary continues from underwater room #11348 north to Underworld3 #30172.

## Keys, leaders, and quest context

The ZON resets place the gatekeeper Brahvinos in #11303 with the small brittle key #11300. The two entrance gate exits name that key. Aracnalis and his apprentices occupy the blood-drawn study #11331; Aracnalis carries adamantium key #11322 for the upper stair door. Arcanusinil carries illusionary key #11321, which matches the locked upward exit into Ximixix's throne room. The mobile and item placement therefore outline a source-level progression, though live access and return have not been played through.

There is no local QST file or local story sidecar. A separate White Harbor story does define a delivery request for “a memory of The Outcasts Tower”: object #55447 is reset in Ximixix's throne room, and White Harbor QST giver #55279 is the Outcasts Tower ambassador. Its story hint identifies the throne room as the recovery location and names a reward of 1,000,000 copper, a gnomish shopkeepers token, and a strange scroll of enchantment ([QST item requirement](../../../areas/qst/wh.qst#L3734), [story contract](../../../areas/story/wh.story.json#L1079), [item source](../../../areas/obj/wh.obj#L5279), [ambassador](../../../areas/mob/wh.mob#L3979)).

Two other foreign objects are reset in the throne room: Heavens rune stone #359 and Jindon psionicist power locket #82018. The rune stone's description speaks of mana nodes across Duris; the locket's description gives it an astral or other-dimensional origin. Ximixix's equipment also includes local black ring #11319, and a shadow dragon follows him. Their ownership and gameplay links are documented in the [audit review](../quest-story-followups/OTOWER_AUDIT_REVIEW.md#external-quest-and-typed-dependencies).

## Connectivity and retention

All 51 local rooms lie in one authored directed component: treating local WLD exits as edges, each room is reachable from and can return to #11300. The 104 Otower exit records all resolve at load; 102 are local and two leave the zone. The reciprocal active boundary at #11300 south reaches Surface #616249. At #11348 north, the route reaches Underworld3 #30172. The steep-trail and drowning language is environmental prose; the WLD census has no fall-chance or current fields.

Several authored routes are doors set by ZON resets, and some reset comments call them secret. The upper levels also depend on the two carried keys above. See the [door-state reconciliation, source-only Surf approach, and migration decision](../quest-story-followups/OTOWER_AUDIT_REVIEW.md#active-graph-door-resets-and-source-only-approach). Keep active otower **DO NOT MOVE** while its cross-zone quest and object dependencies, legacy numeric references, and access questions remain attached to these source identities.
