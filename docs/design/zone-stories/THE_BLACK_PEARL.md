The intended route begins with the scrawled invitation at Tharnadia's Tower of
the Cleric. Warthehr sends adventurers to the Caer Tannad prison thief, the
farmer's son in Twin Towers Forest, Turopolis sewers, the Gartham in Pharr
Valley, Milerg on Evermeet, Jindonium's circus, Derimous in Verspin, and the
Ffolk Lyle/Abal errands. Jyarl in Newhaven supplies the hilt; Telrar in
Kimodril reconstructs the sword. Showing the sword provides a narrated
mountain-inlet/ghost-ship lead to Ghalasax. Her skin earns the gemstones.
This order comes from dialogue, not a native all-stage prerequisite system.

The eight fragment kinds share the name **“a sword fragment”**. Warthehr's
Gartham, hunter and Ffolk reports replace originals 142207/142210/142211 with
142226/142227/142228. Telrar needs those replacements, five other distinct
fragments and the hilt. Eight copies of one kind, the three unexchanged originals,
or a receipt for a piece already spent cannot satisfy the nine-root recipe.
The native limit of fourteen offering roots already admits this exact set.
There is no new general crafting matcher to invent for this zone.

Warthehr's letter **142213** and Abal's reply **142219** are both named a letter
to Lyle, but they feed different native exchanges. The letter to Abal is 142214;
the letter to Jyarl is 142212. A duplicate or same-looking letter cannot stand
in for another stage. The journal labels the producer and intended recipient.

Twenty-five optional checks explain valid preparation: 23 earlier native
receipts and two live key checks. They never take `Next:` priority and cannot
substitute for physical offerings. Telrar accepts a supplied complete set without
the seven producer receipts; Warthehr accepts supplied skin without personal
reconstruction, showing the sword, using it, killing Ghalasax or completing the
royal errand. Each actual hand-in remains an independent fact. The final skin
receipt does not certify the entire campaign. The shared all-stage/branch/reveal
proposal must explicitly define which history, actors and attempts count.

## Wreck access, specials and separate encounters

Room 142201 has the static outward south exit to surface **134956**, a
mountains tile whose north exit leads to 134656. No ordinary reciprocal exit
to 142201 was found. Raw Duris3 references to that numeric map location are
ordinary grid neighbours, not a reviewed dungeon entrance. The separate
Raxquest wreck 70501 also exits to 134956; it is not the Black Pearl entrance.
The map-versus-interior location and final loaded topology need live qualification
before claiming the narrative western path is usable.

The permanent mobile Black Pearl hunter dreadnought is **disabled**.
`load_zone_ship`, its global and the interior linking are commented out;
initialization and combat/AI special cases are also dormant. The setup table,
ship-type support and active prohibition on a player claiming its name do not
spawn the ship or establish an entrance. Re-enabling it requires a coordinated
choice about location/link ownership, ship lifecycle, reset authority, hostile
AI, failure recovery and balance. An explicit static entrance could be a smaller
builder-selected repair. This journal implements neither choice.

The initial route is grating 142201→142202 with rusty key 142240, then north
through heavy wooden door 142245, steel bars 142246 and door 142247 to 142205.
All four keys come from the exitless holding-room spirits. These doors reset
locked; the cargo trapdoor above 142205 and several other cargo doors reset
locked with key zero. The journal does not promise those doors have an obtainable
key, but also does not conclude they are unopenable by every generic pick/spell
route. Review approved lock difficulty, alternate access and confirmed passage.

Two side closets off 142230 reset D6, secret/closed/locked, using small brass
key 142250 carried by an undead sailor in that area. Their reverse exits
reset closed/unlocked. The northern guard route at 142257 uses small bone

`has_key` matches the exact carried/held VNUM without requiring KEY type, so
the unusual type alone does not invalidate it. The roof trapdoor at 142272

Only **two of 84 room descriptions** contain text: the entry and one dank

The similarly named `newbie_quest` and `dragonslayer` procedures in

ordinary item behavior; planned special powers need an explicit builder decision.

## Economics, rewards and campaign ownership

Mode zero does not mean the declarations are never loaded. Normal boot can
reset the area; copyover/recovered-world paths preserve prior populations.
Ordinary periodic reset scheduling excludes mode-zero zones, while explicit
staff/epic reset paths remain separate. Do not turn disappearing campaign
contacts into daily-repeatable givers just because a named foreign destination
normally resets. Current eligibility uses the **logical owning zone's** mode.
The six candidates are Warthehr's three transformations and skin finale,
Lyle's first letter and Abal's first letter. Source/recipient access remains
blocked despite their non-disappearing native definitions.

Fresh active ordinary O/P/G/E item generation still lacks qualified committed
reset authority, including nested and equipped supplies. Existing recovered
items may exist; do not describe the entire deployed world as empty. The patron's
coin-only offering is separately unsupported and has no automatic generic
mixed-offering warning, so its authored service text explicitly explains the
active-accounting refusal. Five platinum equals the native 5,000 copper.

Warthehr's **100,000-copper reward** already uses the native committed wallet
reward path with identified recovery. It is not a fee or a durable-offering gap.
The twelve final gemstones are fixed item rewards to the offering actor, with
capacity-aware item publication; party-size prose does not implement allocation
or an entitlement of one pair per participant. A follow-up party allocation
proposal needs frozen recipients and recoverable individual item entitlements,
without duplicating current shared receipt or experience credit.

Hunter giving has no player-class condition. Class-themed descriptions guide
equipment choice; item anti-flags/wear conditions are independent. All six
native success strings are empty. Large trades specify E1000, small E500;
`E` is experience, subject to native level caps and captured recipient/recovery
policy, not a guaranteed raw grant to every later party member. The same gem
can feed one selected physical trade. The gnome and dwarf share heart 142238,
but their second large-gem equipment differs. Builder text should describe the
actual selected items, gem consumption and bounded experience.

The source owns the campaign as Black Pearl, while intended NPCs live across
other zones. `meet_npc` can persist a contact in any discovered physical room,
but runtime encounter hints use the **current physical area's** journal and
the Black Pearl journal still requires Black Pearl discovery. Thus relocating
Warthehr to Tharnadia alone could retain the encounter while giving a misleading
journal link or hiding his prologue until the wreck is discovered. Native
completion facts can retain logical ownership and physical room separately;
that is already preferable to moving or duplicating receipt IDs.

Add an explicit campaign association/reveal rule: a met foreign contact can
introduce the owned campaign without falsely earning Black Pearl discovery.
Resolve encounter hints to every permitted owning journal, retain physical
discovery as its own achievement and freeze daily policy under the user's
discovered-zone requirement. Define whether pre-discovery native history is
shown after actual discovery, rather than silently unlocking daily eligibility
from conversation. Apply the same rules to terminal group recipients and replay.
