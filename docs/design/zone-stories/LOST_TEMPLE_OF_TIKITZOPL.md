## Actual access, clues and return

Lost City's Sacrificial Leader 43763 accepts kitty 43742 for temple key 43743.
Kitty stock is declared with the insane mage and pet-shop owner. That foreign
key opens the approach between 44038/44039; 44039 DOWN reaches temple 44101.
The entrance's sealed northern door is descriptive scenery without an actual
northern exit. The hidden downward route leads through deeper corridors; its
wall/tapestry secrets and elemental mazes use shared access rather than a
bespoke accepted-keyword quest.

Large golem 44100 carries flaming key 44103 at 44205. It fits the secret,
locked fire-side wall 44209 NORTH toward 44214. Zynar's wooden key opens guards'
entry 44265 EAST / 44291 WEST; master of arms 44151 carries glass component
44122 at training grounds 44295. Glass is TREASURE, not a working KEY.
Gem key 44189 is inside cot 44186 at 44294, not inside the locked rack.
The rack 44184 uses that key and holds two yellow vials. The same key opens
royal entry 44257 SOUTH to 44286. These three local access keys have zero break
chance. Native `has_key` uses the exact carried or held kind.

Royal altar 44100 at 44290 is a `push` switch targeting that room's DOWN exit.
The reset makes it locked, secret and blocked. The target and reciprocal exist;
because the near exit is secret, pushing clears its block without modifying
the reverse exit, lock or secret. Golden key 44101 unlocks the passage to 44296
and has a one-percent break chance. It does not itself supply the Orb.

Beyond the treasure rooms, seven locked speech doors use the last keyword:
`courage`, `beholder`, `deftness`, `fleeting`, `prayer`, `power`, `understanding`.
Shared speech clears locked/secret on valid matching near/reverse exits; opening
and passage remain separate. The last southern edge 44332 → 44333 is already
open and keyless, despite evasion imagery. Do not invent an eighth password.

High Altar 44333 has no ordinary outward exits. Unlocked altar container 44165
holds Orb 44164, cap one. Taking it does not require praying or changing class.
Winterhaven 55632 also enters this room, so the full local key route cannot be
mandatory for every legitimate acquisition. Array 44190 uses `enter`, unlimited
charges, and leads one way to Lost City's Snake Pit 43704. Ground leaf key
43754 opens that room's north exit. Confirmed retrieval, travel and owned source
