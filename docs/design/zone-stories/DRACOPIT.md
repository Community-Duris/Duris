# Pit of Dragons

Active `dracopit` is zone #159, titled “Pit of Dragons,” with 69 rooms (#15900–#15968), 16 mobile prototypes, and 15 object prototypes. It appears in both `areas/AREA` and `areas/qst/AREA`, but has no local QST, shop file, or story sidecar. Its zone has 81 non-`S` commands: 2 door states, 2 room objects, 40 mobile placements, 18 equipment placements, 7 gifts, and 12 follower placements. See the [area list](../../../areas/AREA#L85), [zone header and resets](../../../areas/zon/dracopit.zon#L1), [QST area index](../../../areas/qst/AREA#L78), [rooms](../../../areas/wld/dracopit.wld#L1), [mobile prototypes](../../../areas/mob/dracopit.mob#L1), [object prototypes](../../../areas/obj/dracopit.obj#L1), and [coverage row 78](../quest-story-followups/ZONE_COVERAGE_REGISTER.csv#L78).

## The Pit and its extradimensional pockets

Sixty-two rooms share the “Pit of Dragons” title and environmental description. The source depicts extreme humidity and permanent twilight, with gray mist obscuring sight, no visible ground, and a sense of dread and death. Dragons occupy the misty terrain throughout the large local room component. Two rooms, #15917 and #15940, are titled “An Extradimensional Pocket.” Both describe a tear in reality too small for dragons to enter under normal circumstances and call it the only safety in the Pit; each offers a star map and a sense of tragic beauty. The source establishes a refuge from the dragons but does not explain who made the pockets or what caused the Pit. See the [first pocket](../../../areas/wld/dracopit.wld#L732) and [second pocket](../../../areas/wld/dracopit.wld#L1718).

The reset roster makes the Pit’s inhabitants concrete: gold, white, blue, red, black, and bronze dragons, undead dragon followers, dragon lords, a guardian, and a dragon overlord. The overlord (#15904) is reset in room #15900 wearing a lightning earring and wielding the gold-and-ivory mace Dragondeath. The mace’s description says it is particularly powerful against dragons, and compiled code assigns it the `mace_dragondeath` object special. Other local objects include a blue dragon egg, a white dragon eyelid, bronze dragonscales, black-scale shoulder guards, and a severed tail spike. See the [reset roster](../../../areas/zon/dracopit.zon#L20), [Dragondeath](../../../areas/obj/dracopit.obj#L122), and [special assignment](../../../src/specs/specs.assign.c#L1321).

## A separate marble corridor and a guarded portal

Rooms #15964–#15968 form a second raw WLD component, separate from the 64-room Pit and pocket component. The five rooms are a black-marble hallway carved with dragons. Its source describes the craftsmanship as beautiful but the feeling of fear and dread as almost overpowering. At #15964, the zone places a carved-dragon portal (#15909) and a golden warning plaque (#15910). The portal object is an `ITEM_TELEPORT` targeting #15940, one of the extradimensional pockets. The plaque warns that the most powerful dragons that plagued the realms are imprisoned here and held by a portal through which they cannot pass; it tells visitors that entering means certain death and urges them to turn back. See the [hallway](../../../areas/wld/dracopit.wld#L2747), [portal reset](../../../areas/zon/dracopit.zon#L17), [portal object](../../../areas/obj/dracopit.obj#L158), and [plaque](../../../areas/obj/dracopit.obj#L174).

An active WLD route reaches the hallway from Mountain Castle through #15968. The two local door resets set the north exit from #15967 and the south exit from #15968 closed and locked. Both WLD lock fields name external key #37115, a dragon-tooth key rewarded by Lord Du’Maathe in Mountain Castle. An active Realm exit also leads one way into the main Pit at #15913. An additional active arrival is dynamic rather than a WLD edge: Caertannad resets a burning `ITEM_TELEPORT` portal whose target is room #15907. See the [door resets](../../../areas/zon/dracopit.zon#L7), [key fields](../../../areas/wld/dracopit.wld#L2819), [key object](../../../areas/obj/mntcastl.obj#L220), [quest reward](../../../areas/qst/mntcastl.qst#L135), and [Caertannad portal reset](../../../areas/zon/caertannad.zon#L390).

## Cross-source details to reconcile

The dragon-hide cloak (#15904) has an extended description saying it is “only given to the overlord of the dragons.” At room #15936, however, the zone loads a great black dragon, then an undead dragon follower (#15907), and immediately gives the cloak. The reset handler uses the current mobile pointer for `G` commands, while `F` loads the follower into that pointer, so the authored reset placement is on the follower rather than the overlord. This looks like a source text/reset mismatch. See the [cloak description](../../../areas/obj/dracopit.obj#L69), [reset sequence](../../../areas/zon/dracopit.zon#L72), and [reset handlers](../../../src/world/db.c#L7494).

The Pit’s zone header uses reset mode 0. The staff status display labels that mode “Zone never resets,” and the ordinary startup scheduler skips it; the scheduled event path instead calls a special no-reset handler that can trigger `reset_zone` from persisted zone state. Static source review therefore cannot establish the current runtime reset state, and the authored door states should not be treated as observed live conditions. See the [zone header](../../../areas/zon/dracopit.zon#L1), [status label](../../../src/cmd/staff_stat.c#L2282), [startup scheduler](../../../src/world/new_events.c#L2068), and [special reset path](../../../src/world/events.c#L450).
