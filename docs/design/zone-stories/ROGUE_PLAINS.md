# Rogue Plains: comprehensive source story map

**Source-comprehensive, revision one — October 4, 2026. Gameplay qualification
remains open.** The [journal](../../../areas/story/roguerai.story.json) covers
all nine local exchanges through seven story outcomes. Twenty-four contacts
retain all three addressed dialogue families and include the foreign Balance
referral. Sixteen optional checks comprise thirteen current-material checks
and three producer histories. The cloud and storm giants each have two recipes
for one outcome; no contract is omitted or moved to another zone.

**This checkpoint ships journal guidance and evidence, with no actual native
zone or quest repair.** Reward wording, identical proof names, meat/item-type
intent, Master-set membership and the historical northern connection remain
findings or qualification decisions. Actual future repairs require separate
clear `fix` commits where practical and prominent PR/news entries naming zone,
interaction, player trigger, before/after behavior, validation, limits and a
truthful player-facing news sentence. Proposed repairs are not shipped fixes.

Active, ready accounting is mandatory for discovery, encounters, journals and
new credit. Frozen obligation recovery/persistence remains separate. Supplied
exact proof fits without personal source history. An accepted exchange does not
prove a first acquisition, personal kill, learning a word, opening a container,
climbing, casting silence, raising a dracolich or restoring the entire zone.

## Giants, alternatives and identical promises

The mediator, cloud giant and storm giant start together at summit 75911. Native
mobile descriptions explain the bickering and lost medal; the file supplies no
addressed keyword for those recipients. Journal guidance must not promise a
scripted answer to an invented word.

The stronghold witchdoctor 75836 starts inside yurt interior 75879 and has potion
75850. F-loaded patrol witchdoctor 75841 starts at 75909 and has potion 75851.
Both are real sources; the latter is a follower, and the former can wander.
Each potion kind has a shared cap of one. Both have identical visible names and
the [silence spell](../../../src/magic/spells.h#L139) in their potion values.
Either exact kind satisfies either giant, but the two deliveries consume two
separate objects. A receipt from giving one potion cannot supply the other.

The two promises also have identical visible names/aliases but distinct kinds.
One cloud promise and one storm promise are required together. Two cloud promises
are insufficient even if they look the same. Each producer history accepts either
of that giant's recipes; the two histories are separate optional checks rather
than one OR list masquerading as an AND prerequisite. Supplied matching promises
remain valid without either personal history.

Acceptance records the promise exchange. The native Q path does not invoke a
silence spell or implement permanent quiet, so a potion receipt does not certify
either effect. The mediator's reply says "sword" and the hammer's extra description
says "blade", while its aliases/name/reward identify a hammer. This is a concrete
presentation inconsistency. Agree the intended weapon before a separate native
wording or reward repair; the journal names the actual current reward accurately.

## Buffalo flesh and the departing orc

Ten buffalo placements share prototype 75812. Five G sources declare common
flesh 75837, with shared cap five. Rare kind 75843 comes from one buffalo starting
at 75803, alongside a common kind. Separate buffalo at 75823 have rare kinds
75844 and 75845. Buffalo can wander across boundaries, so those origins are not
permanent current source rooms. A matching appearance is not proof of stock.

The four flesh kinds have identical aliases, visible names and descriptive prose.
They require four independent current checks. Four common strips, or multiple
copies of another kind, cannot substitute. Source labels clarify the difference
in the journal without renaming native objects. A future compatible alias/label
repair should preserve identities and test all four kinds and wrong duplicates.

All four objects currently use ITEM_POTION values `[20,16,0,0]`; spell 16 is
[cure light](../../../src/magic/spells.h#L29). Their prose talks about eating raw
meat. This is a real type/prose mismatch, not proof that the quest exchange itself
is broken: the native delivery matches object kinds and remains defined. Builder
intent could be medicinal flesh, food or flavor text. Do not silently replace
the type/spell or consume it during journal qualification.

The orc starts at 75938, wanders and can leave the zone. Acceptance awards two
identical hides and removes him. Stock/reset-generation and retiring-recipient
episodes need live qualification before claiming a stable daily loop. Hides
mention a trapper, but the active native quest scan finds no hide consumer. Treat
that as absent continuation/decorative intent, not a fabricated next exchange.

## Boots, partial soul, bones and magic

Two sentinels 75819 start at 75873; one E reset equips boots 75832 in the feet
slot. The old sage at 75901 exchanges those boots for only part of his soul.
The reply and object description both explicitly limit the soul. No escort,
actual climb, destination arrival or full-soul transfer is implemented.

Three hands of death start at 75881, 75884 and 75893. Only the last declared
instance has the finger-bones G source. The reaper starts at 75847 and answers
`hi/hello` and `staff`, describing both inputs. One exact partial soul and the
finger bones give reward 75838. The earlier boot receipt is optional supply
history; supplied matching soul remains valid. Consumed, worn or container-held
proof is not restored by an accepted producer receipt.

The reward is named a staff but is ITEM_WAND, level fifty, three charges and
spell 143: [create dracolich](../../../src/magic/spells.h#L155). Its receipt is
not a casting event. A newborn dracolich already has an ordinary M reset in the
bone yard; raising lore and the slab's presence do not define a special ritual
or its causal result. Native type/name intent and any desired successful spell
objective require separate builder/runtime qualification, including targets,
charges, rejection, actual effect and ownership. No magic-effect repair ships.

The spell is [registered for a room object](../../../src/classes/skills.c#L4302).
Its [actual implementation](../../../src/classes/necromancy.c#L1599) requires
a same-room ITEM_CORPSE of level at least 46, a living/mobile caster outside a
safe room, valid form and available undead control capacity. Dragon-scale
consumption is commented out, not a current prerequisite. Globe and skill affect
the result; a hostile awakening differs from a controlled follower. The channeling
[staff-target guard](../../../src/item/device_actions.c#L528) does not accept this
object-only spell as a staff-area target, so blindly changing the item's type to
STAFF is not a safe cosmetic repair. Keep wand targeting unless intended behavior
is explicitly redesigned and tested.

When enabled, [durable corpse raising](../../../src/world/handler.c#L4657) uses
separate NPC-room or player-corpse custody transactions and only publishes the
follower through the accepted path. A device action and that corpse transformation
are separate stages, with admission/rejection, hostile versus controlled outcome,
pet identity and restart/recovery boundaries. Future journal objectives need the
actual accepted transformation and desired control state, not a device-charge
receipt or the already reset newborn dracolich. This records existing dependency
semantics; neither a new spell defect nor a new objective is claimed.

## Master sigil and real effect boundaries

Keva wears sigil 75857 in room 75949. An alternative foreign G reset on Sorem-Krell
in `vehicles` has a twenty-percent roll and the same cap one. Existing ownership
can suppress stock at another source. The direct assignment runs
[master_set](../../../src/specs/specs.set.c#L62), which schedules periodic checks
and only acts on a worn item belonging to an alive player during CMD_PERIODIC.

The legacy list has seven members: 22063, 22237, 22621, 45530, 45531, 82545 and
75857. It counts matching occupied worn slots, not distinct prototypes, and caps
at six. It removes/replaces TAG_SET_MASTER and grants thresholds at two pieces
(armor −20), three (spell save −3), four (hitroll +5), five (detect invisible)
and six (haste). One sigil alone does not meet the first bonus threshold.

The [separate set adapter table](../../../src/specs/specs.set_equipment.c#L419)
also lists 82559 and its dispatcher deduplicates prototypes. The sigil's actual
assignment is the direct legacy function, whose adapter ignores the count
argument; therefore do not claim that eight-member table or deduplication governs
this sigil. Membership/counting intent needs review. Unequip/last-piece removal,
death, ownership transfer, periodic timing and restart lifecycle require executed
effect qualification before calling them bugs or awarding a set objective.

Future integration needs committed equip/unequip plus actual accepted effect
events, stable set identity/version and precise membership/threshold semantics.
Current equipped-item readiness can describe possession, but cannot certify a
persisted full-set effect or personal first recovery. No new set achievement or
unqualified lifecycle repair ships with this journal.
