# Valley of Crushk builder questions and recommendations

The [source-comprehensive dossier](VALLEY_CRUSHK.md) and [Checkpoint 172
audit review](../quest-story-followups/VALLEY_CRUSHK_AUDIT_REVIEW.md) map one
native Flazoh bounty and broader woodland, gate, faerie, village, and route
material. This checklist makes twenty direct-source builder decisions explicit.
It keeps the native item exchange distinct from the larger story suggestions
and does not certify installed or played completion. See [coverage row 334](../quest-story-followups/ZONE_COVERAGE_REGISTER.csv#L334).
