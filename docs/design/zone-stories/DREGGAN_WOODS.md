# Dreggan Woods

## At a glance

Dreggan Woods is a 188-room, foggy forest maze whose repeated room text says that its paths twist the visitor’s sense of reality and make it hard to remember where they came from. The entrance warns that evil taunts people into the woods, with shadows and howls at the edge of the forest. Nearly every room uses the same title; a few variants mention fog and evil, but the WLD gives the interior no named landmarks.

The zone’s reset roster is an undead force: a zombie lord, lesser and greater zombies, skeletal knights, and massive worms. The lord says it is ordering its minions; its description says it was once a large human or perhaps a giant. The knights were noble knights before becoming undead. The source depicts the forest as threatening but leaves the identity and origin of its evil unstated. See the [entrance and maze text](../../../areas/wld/dreggan_wood.wld#L1), [mobile roster](../../../areas/mob/dreggan_wood.mob), and [reset placements](../../../areas/zon/dreggan_wood.zon#L15).

## The Ethereal Plane, the maze, and Shadamehr Keep

The active WLD connects Dreggan’s entrance, room #41001, reciprocally with the Ethereal Plane at #12480. Another reciprocal boundary links Dreggan #41186 north to the entrance of Shadamehr Keep at #41200. All 188 rooms form one weakly connected local graph, and there are no door flags, local D resets, fall fields, or current fields. The authored directions are not fully reversible: all-exit directed reach from Ethereal arrival #41001 covers all 188 rooms, while arrival from Shadamehr at #41186 reaches 89. This is a static description of source exits, not a tested travel or escape route. See the [Ethereal entrance](../../../areas/wld/dreggan_wood.wld#L1), [Shadamehr boundary](../../../areas/wld/dreggan_wood.wld#L5024), and [connectivity audit](../quest-story-followups/DREGGAN_WOOD_AUDIT_REVIEW.md#connectivity-and-reset-inventory).

An adventuring parchment in active Brass gives directions from the Void in Time into Dreggan, then lists a route through the woods to Shadamehr Keep. The note reinforces the authored connection between the confusing wood and the keep, although the route itself has not been walked. See [Shanaanaa’s notes](../../../areas/obj/brass.obj#L1355).

## Flowers and the Alatorin exchange

The Dreggan ZON places four ethereal lilies (#83284) in separate rooms. Alatorin’s mystic gardener says that these lilies are found only in the murky Dreggan Woods. Her exchange asks for four distinct ethereal flowers, including the lily, and gives the ethereal symbiote cloak (#83285). Alatorin also resets a lily onto Mishanen at a 2% chance; its story sidecar records this as her declared carried supply. The local #83284 placements and the rare giver stock are both present in source, while actual availability depends on reset state. See the [Dreggan flower resets](../../../areas/zon/dreggan_wood.zon#L9), [gardener’s dialogue and exchange](../../../areas/qst/alatorin.qst#L3628), and [item contract](../../../areas/story/alatorin.story.json#L1607).

Dreggan also resets a local Cronus Flower (#41004) in room #41059. Its two local flower prototypes describe blooming (#41004) and growing silently (#41005). The hourly weather event looks for one of these objects in #41059 and swaps #41004 to #41005 at MUD hour 23, then back at hour 1. The local rusty key (#41003) is not reset inside Dreggan; Shadamehr Keep resets it on Merkus and uses it for the paired vault door. See the [Cronus Flower objects](../../../areas/obj/dreggan_wood.obj#L9), [weather handler](../../../src/world/weather.c#L53), [key object](../../../areas/obj/dreggan_wood.obj#L1), and [Shadamehr key reset and door](../quest-story-followups/DREGGAN_WOOD_AUDIT_REVIEW.md#typed-dependencies-and-source-only-references).
