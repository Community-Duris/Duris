# The Towering Glacier of Dayedenvale

This source dossier covers the active Glacier contributor: 105 rooms (#90401–#90505), ten mobile prototypes, and nine object prototypes. The reset, route, dependency, title, and history review is in the [Glacier audit supplement](../quest-story-followups/GLACIER_AUDIT_REVIEW.md).

## Ice above a freezing sea

The exterior rooms describe floating ice mounds bobbing in violent, freezing water beneath an arch of glaciers. Wind-beaten ledges cling to the glacier above pounding waves. The entrance at #90452 leads from that exposed coast into a dark cavern, with matted, oil-soiled fur caught around its threshold ([ice mound](../../../areas/wld/glacier.wld#L1), [entrance](../../../areas/wld/glacier.wld#L1111), [base](../../../areas/wld/glacier.wld#L1342)).

The cave becomes a foul den. At #90469, stripped prey, steaming dung, and frozen blood cover a kill and feasting ground. Deeper passages are slippery and lined with filth and hair. A concealed block of ice opens into the Chamber of Blood, whose red mineral walls surround rancid flesh, rotting bones, and a warm breeze ([kill pile](../../../areas/wld/glacier.wld#L1460), [concealed door](../../../areas/wld/glacier.wld#L1890), [Chamber of Blood](../../../areas/wld/glacier.wld#L2067)).

## Falina and the locked room

At the chamber’s end, a slab separates room #90504 from a quiet, lit room furnished with a bed, chair, table, and dishes. Its text calls the space lived-in but does not identify who made it so ([slab](../../../areas/wld/glacier.wld#L2136), [room #90505](../../../areas/wld/glacier.wld#L2154)).

The reset roster places Princess Falina in #90505 with a deviant cuffsman. Her mobile text describes her sobbing, carrying years of anguish, and appearing to face an internal conflict ([Falina](../../../areas/mob/glacier.mob#L85), [resets](../../../areas/zon/glacier.zon#L94)). The same resets place Vishava the vampiress at #90408 with her locket ([Vishava reset](../../../areas/zon/glacier.zon#L26), [locket](../../../areas/obj/glacier.obj#L99)). Bears, wolves, huskies, pups, and stone cuffsmen populate the coast and caves.

The locked slab names object #90402 as its key. That object is labeled “Falina’s Soul” and “Falina’s Eternal Soul.” A ZON give command for it follows the King Polar spawn at #90447; the reset loader’s G command gives the object to the current mobile ([key](../../../areas/obj/glacier.obj#L19), [King Polar and key resets](../../../areas/zon/glacier.zon#L35), [G reset handling](../../../src/world/db.c#L7574)). This creates a source-level link among Falina, the key, and the locked room. There is no local QST file or recorded objective and outcome, so the source does not establish what a player is meant to do or whether Falina is rescued.

## Source boundary

Both the full area roster and QST roster list Glacier. The local QST source file and SHP file are absent, and there is no story sidecar or native Q/QA contract. This dossier describes checked-in text and reset instructions; it does not establish loaded reset state, door or key availability, or live gameplay.
