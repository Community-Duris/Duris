# The Scorched Valley: comprehensive source story map

**Source-comprehensive, revision one — October 3, 2026. Gameplay qualification
remains open.** The [sidecar](../../../areas/story/scorchvalley.story.json)
explains all nine native deliveries as independent outcomes, with twenty-two
contacts, all eleven addressed dialogue families and twenty-two optional
material, key or producer-history checks. Nine potential daily candidates
describe contract shape; they do not guarantee live supply, access or payout.

**This checkpoint ships journal guidance and evidence, with no actual native
zone or quest repair.** Yeenoghu's disconnected special dispatch, the
commander-head clue and the council advisor's departure text have explicit
repair plans below. They must remain pending in PR/news reporting until a
separate identifiable fix and appropriate qualification ship.

Active, ready accounting is mandatory for discovery, encounters, journals and
new credit. All nine offerings contain items only, and all native recipients
stay after acceptance. A key, earlier receipt or clue cannot supply spent
proof, restore a killed recipient, establish a personal recovery or finish
an all-stage campaign. Frozen obligations retain their separate recovery path.
