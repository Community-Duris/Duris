# The Fortress of Dreams

## At a glance

Active eth2 is zone #326, titled “The Fortress of Dreams,” with 111 rooms (#32600–#32710), 45 mobile prototypes, and 42 object prototypes. The ZON header uses reset mode 0 and declares 158 non-S commands: 61 M, 12 O, 14 G, 27 E, 14 F, and 30 D. Both the active area roster and QST/AREA list eth2. No local QST, SHP file, story sidecar, or native Q/QA contract is present. ## From the Ethereal planes to the fortress

The first rooms cross the Deep Ethereal and Border Ethereal, then descend through a Vortex to a black-obsidian plain beneath yellow fog. Ashahel's stairway climbs to the Fortress of Dreams, described as Aramus's home. Its entryway looks abandoned, while the rooms beyond contain spectral mirrors, a chamber of books, damp slaadi halls, fiery zealots, a ghostly cathedral, and the throne room. Aramus's room calls him lord of the Ethereal Plane and says he also controls the Otherworld of Dreams. See the [Vortex and obsidian approach](../../../areas/wld/eth2.wld#L1669), [fortress entrance](../../../areas/wld/eth2.wld#L1769), [entryway](../../../areas/wld/eth2.wld#L1814), [mirror room](../../../areas/wld/eth2.wld#L1890), [slaadi throne room](../../../areas/wld/eth2.wld#L2102), and [Aramus's throne room](../../../areas/wld/eth2.wld#L2836).

Ashahel carries the small brass key #32604, which matches both sides of the locked brass entrance between #32660 and #32661. Deeper locks use local items carried by reset mobiles: a pendant on Yves, a bone key on a ravager assassin, a wooden key on the lost girl, a mold-encrusted key on a death slaad, and a rainbow key on Aramus. The last opens the locked door from the throne room to Aramus's treasure room. The treasure-room prose calls its gold, weapons, and jewels illusions. Its reset also places a portal, equipment, and Heavens monolith #360. See the [door declarations](../../../areas/zon/eth2.zon#L8), [key resets](../../../areas/zon/eth2.zon#L168), [rainbow key and Aramus](../../../areas/zon/eth2.zon#L255), [treasure room](../../../areas/wld/eth2.wld#L2860), and [monolith prototype](../../../areas/obj/heavens.obj#L3741).

## A forest where dreams turn hostile

Another branch describes an ethereal forest, an ancient tree, and a little lost girl. The zone resets jodani and white-oak tree objects in the woods; their compiled handler can make those objects stir into hostile tree mobiles when a player goes north in the room. The mobile file defines the lost girl as #32627, and the object file defines the ethereal rose with the same VNUM. Source handlers replace the girl with a demon princess on death and can turn the death of forest animals into nightmare demons. These behaviors are source-defined; no gameplay outcomes were observed. See the [forest and tree rooms](../../../areas/wld/eth2.wld#L2319), [tree and rose placements](../../../areas/zon/eth2.zon#L133), [lost-girl reset](../../../areas/zon/eth2.zon#L242), [behavior assignments](../../../src/specs/specs.assign.c#L2607), and [Ethereal handlers](../../../src/specs/specs.eth2.c#L27).
