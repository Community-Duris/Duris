## Authored setting and story threads

The authored route descends beside a swift, cold river and waterfall, through
a cave and a lava passage, then reaches Valinhaven's illuminated gates. The
room text portrays the settlement as an old svirfneblin mining and trading
community built among gem-lined tunnels. It includes a refinery, shops that
are now empty or described as selling nothing, public baths, a kitchen, a
mess hall, a bank, and a miner break room. Those room names and descriptions
establish past community uses; they do not define current services or a
player's route through the settlement. [River and waterfall](../../../areas/wld/valinhav.wld#L1)
