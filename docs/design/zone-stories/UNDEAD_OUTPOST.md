## Source review closure

The narrative pass reviewed all 121 room records, 19 mobile prototypes, 11
object prototypes, and local reset commands. The source traces the former
trading outpost's decline, undead and animal animation, the well's unexplained
necromantic event, Frenbar's hideout and the assassin's finger clue, and
service roles that remain in a place with no local SHP or QST file. The well
story has no authored local resolution contract, and the room names do not
establish that the former merchant or bank still offers a service. The
questions below preserve those distinctions; access, current stock, and
encounter outcomes remain unverified.
