# The Lost Realms of Shadowfall builder questions

Canonical key: `area:wolfen`. Physical coverage row 348. The
[source review](WOLFEN_STORY_REVIEW.md) and
[technical audit](../quest-story-followups/WOLFEN_AUDIT_REVIEW.md) preserve
the current map, lore, and partial outbreak notes without adding a cure,
identity, or mission outcome.

## Explicit builder questions and recommended resolutions

1. **Is present-day Shadowfall the hidden Haven in the history book?** The book
   says Lionel made Haven immaterial and invisible, while current room text
   describes a visible village. **Recommend** keep those places distinct until
   a builder establishes a timeline or identity link.
2. **What does “The Lost Realms” refer to in the current title?** The local
   history mentions an invisible former city but never names it Shadowfall.
   **Recommend** retain the title without rewriting the book's place names.
3. **Is priest Celicus Fahrn the explorer Celicus in the old history?** The
   name appears in both sources, with no age, lineage, or reincarnation link.
   **Recommend** do not identify them as the same person from name alone.
4. **Is Vost's Answerer one of Llevron Dor's nine swords?** Vost carries the
   local longsword #18000, but the book gives none of the swords' names.
   **Recommend** avoid treating the item as a historical relic or proof until
   the intended identity is explicit.
5. **What exactly is the “burning”?** The paper gives the village's name for
   the sensation and records sickness, but no authoritative diagnosis.
   **Recommend** keep the term and its cause separate.
6. **Who is the sick creature in the paper?** Its name is obscured, and the
   nearby Vermithrax description does not establish that he is the subject.
   **Recommend** preserve the blank instead of assigning the dragon.
7. **Is Vermithrax a victim, attacker, or unrelated danger?** The mobile is
   degenerated and covered by strange lightning, with no source tying him to
   the outbreak's cause. **Recommend** keep his role unresolved.
8. **Who destroyed the bridge, and when?** The bridge is blasted apart and a
   paper fragment attributes the damage to an unnamed creature.
   **Recommend** record the destroyed state without inventing a culprit,
   repair, or event order.
9. **Should Vost's directed-attack theory be canonical?** The paper attributes
   that suspicion to Vost and also mentions a glimpse of another creature.
   **Recommend** preserve it as his interpretation until corroborated.
10. **Does the Tree of Life repel the burning, cure it, or simply remain
    healthy?** The druid tends the tree and the paper says it keeps “them” away.
    **Recommend** distinguish protection, cure, and healthy scenery.
11. **What treatment did Jhary try?** The fragment deliberately hides the
    method and says the attempt failed. **Recommend** retain the failed attempt
    and unreadable gap; do not add a potion reward or successful cure.
12. **Did the village kill all infected residents?** The paper says there was
    “no choice” but to kill those infected, but records no result or count.
    **Recommend** treat it as the writer's stated policy, not proof of a
    completed purge.
13. **What followed Estidan's hunting stop and the low stores?** Those are
    authored conditions, not stock or economy state in the zone.
    **Recommend** do not promise scarcity or restoration without a defined
    gameplay consequence.
14. **Who attempted the final portal escape, and who were the companions?**
    Both identities are hidden by literal placeholder text.
    **Recommend** keep the event anonymous until the builder supplies the
    missing names.
15. **Which portal was destroyed in that note?** The current ZON resets two
    paired rifts, while the note says a portal was destroyed.
    **Recommend** establish whether the note is earlier, refers to another
    portal, or is stale; do not declare either live rift destroyed.
16. **Are the collapsed-tunnel slab switches intended to provide both
    ingress and egress?** The two objects say “push,” and their switch data
    targets paired tunnel exits. **Recommend** preserve the paired directions
    and qualify the command and runtime reset state separately.
17. **What travel and return outcome should the two rifts guarantee?** The
    objects target #18002 and #18003, but text about instability complicates
    the portal story. **Recommend** document the source endpoints and keep
    actual travel, party, and return claims unqualified until decided.
18. **Is the wagon portal currently meant to admit travelers while its owner
    awaits tunnel repair?** The wagon object invites entry and targets its
    interior; the merchant resets there. **Recommend** clarify the repair
    wording and preserve return through the authored west exit.
19. **Who may buy from Aliester, and when is stock available?** The shop lists
    three local instruments and has an explicit refusal message.
    **Recommend** keep the authored restriction separate from claims about
    live stock or current character eligibility.
20. **Are Karig's two QST actions intended as ambient lines or as a player
    mission?** They describe silent-environment spellcasting but contain no
    item handoff or completion proof. **Recommend** treat them as authored
    actions only unless a separate contract and outcome are designed.
