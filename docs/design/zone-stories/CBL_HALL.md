# CBL Hall: Clan BloodLust guildhall and portal chambers

## Source identity and authored story

`cbl_hall` is an active full-world area contributor at zone 297–298, titled “The Guildhall of Clan BloodLust.” Its 124 rooms are #29700–#29824. It defines eight mobiles (#29700–#29703 and #29820–#29823), fourteen local objects (#29700–#29713), and two shops (#29701 and #29703). The main area list includes it at [line 143](../../../areas/AREA#L143); the independent quest index also lists it at [line 133](../../../areas/qst/AREA#L133). There is no same-stem `areas/qst/cbl_hall.qst`, native Q/QA contract, or story sidecar.

The WLD text presents a guildhall built as a collage of clan pride, in-jokes, memorial spaces, portals, and parody shrines. The opening rooms include the Goodie-named room, Bloodied Skull inn, trophy board, and clan hall ([rooms #29700–#29703](../../../areas/wld/cbl_hall.wld#L1)). The guildhall prose frames members as elite underdark usurpers. Nearby scenes include a pool, Goodie-donation store room, meditation chamber, beach, cheese factory, and the Meadow of Fallen Warriors ([#29705–#29717](../../../areas/wld/cbl_hall.wld#L106), [#29717](../../../areas/wld/cbl_hall.wld#L307)). A second cluster shifts to the Blood-Soaked Arena, Great Hall, Citadel of the Sword, throne, sanctum, and vault ([#29730–#29739](../../../areas/wld/cbl_hall.wld#L476)). Other sections collect continent/underdark portals and joke shrines for 83, waffles, pancakes, frogs, Batman, and feta cheese. The later rooms stage Du’Maathe, an Alliance of Darkness outpost, a desecrated mausoleum, Netherworld, dark altar, Hall of Lost Souls, Heart of Onda, and Inn of Nightmares ([#29800–#29819](../../../areas/wld/cbl_hall.wld#L1306), [#29810–#29819](../../../areas/wld/cbl_hall.wld#L1428)). The final five rooms lead through Monster Island gates, a torture chamber, pit, narrow stair, and portal cave ([#29820–#29824](../../../areas/wld/cbl_hall.wld#L1629)).

The source documents environmental setting through a non-linear collection of scenes and defines no local quest progression. The QST index membership does not correspond to a local contract file. The source has explicit sexualized scenes, insult-heavy anti-Goodie jokes, and parody religion. Those are content-owner review questions; this audit does not rewrite them.

## Static connectivity and compiled references

The only active boundary pair is CBL #29703 D4/up to Heavens #420 and the reciprocal Heavens #420 D5/down to #29703 ([CBL hall](../../../areas/wld/cbl_hall.wld#L74), [Heavens](../../../areas/wld/heavens.wld#L631)). The local exits have no nonzero door flags or keys; there are no fall/current rooms. Runtime or object-based links are excluded from that reach count.

Two direct C++ references matter to retention. The arena code assigns Evil’s hometown/return location to #29700, whose WLD title is “The Goodie Bitchin Room” ([arena table](../../../src/combat/arena.c#L52), [room title](../../../areas/wld/cbl_hall.wld#L1)). The title/return-name pairing and the portal destinations should be checked with the feature owner before any related content change.

A whole inactive-WLD scan found one non-overlapping source-only approach into CBL: unregistered `ud_map2.wld` room #1124947 has D5/down to active #29703 ([source room](../../../areas/wld/ud_map2.wld#L59101), [exit record](../../../areas/wld/ud_map2.wld#L59122)). ## Duplicate content and bounded history

The normalized active `.zon` title is unique among the 351 active contributors. Followed CBL Hall WLD history reaches the 2009 source import and same-stem 2011/2013 edits; MOB, OBJ, ZON, and SHP history also remains on the `cbl_hall` stem after import. No alternate-stem rename, distinct predecessor, or whole-zone replacement is established.

Two unregistered sources, `Monsteri` and `cbl_outpost2`, share the separate title “CBL Guildhall Monster Island.” Both reuse room IDs #1000–#1004 and mobile IDs #1000–#1003, and their ZON files place the same four mobile roles in room #1000. `Monsteri`’s five normalized room descriptions match the final five active CBL room descriptions at #29820–#29824; four room titles match exactly. The CBL mobile lineup appears corresponding, but exact mobile-prototype equality and intended source precedence have not been established. Both copies remain outside `areas/AREA`, and no active WLD edge targets #1000–#1004. The sources show duplicate content; tracked history does not establish that one file replaced the other. See [candidate row 24](../quest-story-followups/ATTIC_CANDIDATE_DEPENDENCY_REGISTER.csv#L24), [candidate row 72](../quest-story-followups/ATTIC_CANDIDATE_DEPENDENCY_REGISTER.csv#L72), and [the duplicate-history checkpoint](../quest-story-followups/DUPLICATE_AND_REPLACED_ZONES.md#checkpoint-199-cbl-hall-and-monster-island-copies).
