remains open.** The [sidecar](../../../areas/story/snogres.story.json) explains

## Sources, quantities and accounting qualification

The death knight 87704 at corridor 87763 wears claymore 87710 and carries
greatsword 87711. They are two distinct required kinds, rather than two generic
cold-iron weapons. Four remorhaz declarations occupy 87747, 87752, 87754 and
87759; only the first, third and fourth receive hide 87725. All three hide
declarations cap the prototype at three, against a six-copy recipe. The fourth
worm is not automatically a fourth material source. F changes the current
mobile for subsequent E/G commands just as M does.

The whole active inventory has no additional reset or native quest net producer
for the hide. An old `brass-old-1` declaration is outside `areas/AREA`, so it
does not establish active supply. The lich's same-kind refusal cannot multiply
stock. These facts expose a concrete supply mismatch; they do not prove that
every possible retained historical or supplied copy is absent. Builders should
choose sufficient intended source quantity/caps and test replacement, retained
stock, exact six-item allocation and consumption before changing the recipe.

Sundew stalk 87719 has no active G/E/O/P or native reward producer. Ordinary
87712 sundews are plentiful and giant 87735 has a 15 percent declaration, but
neither carries this object. No assigned procedure or source reference issues
it. The shared harvest trigger is bound to kingdom resource nodes, not an
automatic sundew skinning path; no generic skinning dispatch was found that
would fill this gap. Choose an intended sundew/source episode and add accepted
production, rather than claiming killing any plant supplies the reagent.

Leppts 87742 has a 100 percent Surface [reset](../../../areas/zon/surface.zon#L1735),
cap one, in dispersal room 660001. That room has ten outward directions and
his prototype lacks sentinel/stay-zone restrictions; a fixed public encounter
room is not guaranteed. His contract owner remains Snow Ogres 877. The player
must discover the actual physical area before encounter publication; owner
discovery is separate. Explain opening the already discovered valley journal
explicitly. The existing physical-area hint can miss foreign owner guidance;
the shared referral improvement recorded for Scorched Valley also applies here.

Native reset mode zero is not proof that stock never renews. The
[reset event](../../../src/world/events.c#L424) checks age/empty conditions and
otherwise uses [no-reset handling](../../../src/world/db.c#L3222), including
epic completion and database reset percentage. Runtime lifespan/percentage
were not queried. Qualification must cover actual accepted generation and
renewal, including rare recipients and sources, rather than treating static
chance or mode alone as a guarantee.

Under required active accounting, the shared
[reset guard](../../../src/world/db.c#L3333) refuses legacy G/E/O/P issuance
because it lacks durable generation identity. This covers the trophies, hides,
weapons and grotto switch declarations too. Keep that guard. Implement accepted
source generation and recovery before qualifying available stock; do not
disable accounting to demonstrate the journal. Mixed fee settlement additionally
needs atomic material/coin allocation, reward preflight, replay and recovery.
