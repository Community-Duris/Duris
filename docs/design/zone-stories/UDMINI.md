# The Minizones of the Underdark

Active udmini is zone #885, titled The Minizones of the Underdark. It has 21 rooms (#88500–#88520), four mobile prototypes (#88500–#88503), and four object prototypes (#88500–#88503). It is listed in areas/AREA and areas/qst/AREA, although there is no local QST file, shop, or story sidecar. See the [area list](../../../areas/AREA#L312), [zone header](../../../areas/zon/udmini.zon#L1), [QST area index](../../../areas/qst/AREA#L278), and [coverage row 322](../quest-story-followups/ZONE_COVERAGE_REGISTER.csv#L322).

## A glowing mushroom and a corpse full of larvae

The source contains two distinct settings. Rooms #88500–#88501 are inside a giant mushroom stem and cap. The walls glow softly; the upper hollow narrows below the cap, where spores grow in the gills. The other component starts inside a bloated, decaying corpse placed over a cave system. Rooms #88503–#88520 describe narrow, dark caves with half-digested remains and insects; resets place growing fire larvae and vicious fire beetles in three rooms. See the [mushroom rooms](../../../areas/wld/udmini.wld#L1), [corpse and cave entrance](../../../areas/wld/udmini.wld#L31), [cave rooms](../../../areas/wld/udmini.wld#L50), [mobiles](../../../areas/mob/udmini.mob#L1), and [zone resets](../../../areas/zon/udmini.zon#L9).

The area title says “Minizones,” and the WLD graph reflects two separate components: a two-room mushroom branch and a 19-room corpse-and-larvae branch. Both active WLD arrivals land in the larger cave component. No active WLD route enters the mushroom rooms.

## Spores and their outside requests

The local reset places gills object #88501 in room #88501 and nests spores object #88502 inside it. The spores are dark dust. The object prototype #88500 is described as a giant mushroom and is also an enterable ITEM_TELEPORT to room #88500, but no local or outside reset references the object. It does not currently bridge the isolated mushroom rooms into the active arrival graph. The gills object also has raw trap fields 512, 0, 10, and 60; the source does not explain their intended effect. See the [object definitions](../../../areas/obj/udmini.obj#L1) and [object resets](../../../areas/zon/udmini.zon#L9).

The exact spores object has two active outside QST consumers. The Myconid alchemist accepts object #88502 and returns 50,000 currency units; Maerg in Newbie accepts the same item and returns a small magical jar, object #29291. Newbie also has its own reset placing object #88502 on the drow mother in room #29275. The Newbie sidecar describes this as an imported prototype with a local source and an optional way to obtain the jar for Foet’s separate request. These are separate consumers and reset sources; they do not establish that players can reach the isolated mushroom branch or that one item completes both exchanges. See the [Myconid QST](../../../areas/qst/myconid.qst#L1), [Myconid story audit](../quest-story-followups/MYCONID_AUDIT_REVIEW.md#L22), [Newbie reset](../../../areas/zon/newbie.zon#L361), [Maerg’s QST exchange](../../../areas/qst/newbie.qst#L232), and [Newbie story mapping](../../../areas/story/newbie.story.json#L511).

## Entrances, falling, and the return crack

Active WLD boundaries connect the corpse caves to two neighboring areas. ConnectorZones room #54239 exits down to local room #88502; local #88502 exits up to ConnectorZones #54194. Kvarkpass Connector room #54811 exits up to local #88515. The active boundary counts are two inbound, one outbound, and no direct reciprocal pair. Local #88515 exits up to #88508, and the reset places large-crack object #88503 there. Entering that item targets Kvarkpass room #54811.

Room #88502 and room #88520 each declare F 100. The loader stores this as a 100% chance-fall value, and entry handling rolls the value before starting the falling routine. For an ordinary grounded character who does not avoid or catch the fall, open downward exits carry a fall from #88502 through #88520 to #88503. The return item targets #54811, which has its own F 100 field and a downward path through #54832. The WLD links describe authored directions; they do not prove a character can use the route safely or stop at the teleport destination. See [local fall rooms](../../../areas/wld/udmini.wld#L31), [Kvarkpass vertical chamber](../../../areas/wld/kvarkpass_connector.wld#L4507), the [fall chance loader](../../../src/world/db.c#L1395), [entry handling](../../../src/world/handler.c#L1581), and [falling policy](../../../src/world/falling.c#L113).
