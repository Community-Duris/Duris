# Home of the Divine: comprehensive source story map

**Source-comprehensive, revision one — October 3, 2026. Gameplay qualification
remains open.** The [sidecar](../../../areas/story/divhome.story.json) explains
all 32 native exchanges as twenty independent outcomes and twelve preparation
or access services, with 27 contacts, all nineteen useful addressed families
and 48 optional material/history checks. The twentieth raw M family is an
empty bounty default. Twenty potential daily candidates describe contract
shape, not verified availability: one bounty names a missing reward.
**Actual native zone or quest repairs in this checkpoint: none.** Pending
repairs and accounting work are distinct from shipped improvements.

Active, ready accounting is mandatory for discovery, encounters, journals and
new credit. Six mixed item/coin recipes remain guarded. Item-only offerings
with cash rewards already have a durable wallet-reward path; a reward is not
a fee. Generic dragon-scale issuance is separately disabled with accounting
active. Optional service history does not pay fees, supply spent items or
establish a personal campaign.

100 and reserved arguments zero. Zone 407 uses mode-one empty-zone resets.
The shop header is `#40712~`; a numeric-header-only scan would miss it.

No literal local special assignment or additional custom local quest terminal
was found. Review included local identifiers/names, global material sources,
shared native acceptance/reward recovery, item teleporters, doors, containers,
traps, falling, NPC wandering, shop/forge and epic teacher initialization.
The [smith table](../../../src/economy/tradeskill.c#L202) lists Emition's
seven forge choices, and [shop dispatch](../../../src/economy/shop.c#L2268)

Airship Graveyard, Harpy Heights, Moria, Jade Empire, `heavens`, `spshold`,
surface/vehicles/Alatorin and Mini Zones. It does not certify those entire zones

## Material, access and episode facts

The ordinary exterior route is mountain room 615667 down → outside room
40780, with 40780 up returning. Seven infinite-charge ITEM_TELEPORT declarations
provide 40700↔40780 arrival portals, 40758↔40773 Shiva pools,
40756↔40781 loom passages and 40787→foreign Great Below 22053.
Portals/pools use `enter`; looms use **`touch` (command 320)**. The fiery
portal is not keyed by Raith's eyepiece. The shared
[teleport handler](../../../src/magic/spell_travel.c#L949) checks actual command,
target, charge and actor restrictions; [movement](../../../src/magic/spell_travel.c#L714)
does not itself return an accepted semantic quest event. All local positive
reset, room and teleporter destinations inspected resolve. Administrative
22649→40717 is not an ordinary entry claim.

Wispy key 40740 opens the locked palace edge 40748↔40751. Cloudy key 40745
opens both locked prison cells, 40781↔40782 and 40781↔40812. Twenty-two
D declarations include closed, locked and secret-closed edges. Lenia's
requested release has no corresponding persisted release/escort terminal.
The carved statue is eastern-cell ground treasure; the captive demon and
his flaming claw are in the western cell. The claw's T2/type-fire, trigger-2
get/put trap has one charge at level twenty. Successful key custody, opening,
travel, trap survival and first item recovery are different facts.

The siren's four tokens have one-copy worn sources on the four chancellors
in elemental shrines. Riser is worn by the cloud giant leader in 40797,
cap one; a shard lies in 40717, cap one. Starting both weapon branches needs
**two distinct Riser UIDs and two distinct shard UIDs**, plus claw, icicle,
generic scale and 300000 copper. Mode-one reset replenishment, global caps,
already owned copies and competing hammer/weapon consumers require episode
qualification; a single current copy or historic receipt cannot fill both.

Generic scale 392 exists in active `heavens.obj`, but has no static reset or
native Q producer. Its [dragon-death source](../../../src/combat/fight.c#L2178)
requires accounting **inactive** and a dragon with positive XP. Preserve
that deliberate guard until recoverable custom-source issuance is integrated.
Local scales 40735/40736 and foreign jade 76697 do not substitute. The jade
wyrm declaration is one-copy at 25%; local smoke/celestial scales have exact
carried sources. The celestial armor also has a foreign Myrabolus producer;
that does not make Reln's service receipt a necessary material prerequisite.

Wicks's ivory is P content of statue 40703 in 40738, despite three same-named
angelic statues. Feather is P content of nest 40743 in 40811; disc is P content
of anvil 40714 in 40757. These containers start closed (value flag five), not
locked. Global P-reset selection needs actual parent UID/location qualification;
the declaration does not prove placement in the player's present local copy.
Ground treasures include horseshoe 40734 in 40788, egg 40748 in 40807 and
bone statue 40750 in 40782. Rare feather also has five Mini Zones griffon
declarations (20/20/100/100/40%); foreign Harpy egg lies in 31210, while
exotic egg has surface, vehicle and Alatorin alternatives. Moria set lies
in 99001. Exact names are not enough to collapse these kinds.

Five local rooms have fall declarations: 40747/40760 at 30%, 40768 at 40%,
40788 at 50% and 40811 at 25%; each has a declared downward route, including
the secret-closed edge beneath 40768. Foreign Harpy egg room 31210 has 60%.
Flight/levitation/climbing, actual passage and survival must be qualified
before adding personal access or recovery objectives.

Raith carries his heart in 40787. Snent carries his in the mist at 40713 and
is also an encrust teacher. Vain, Gravet and Relazier start in Rare Load Two
40803; Ajandaner starts in Rare Load Three 40813. The holding groups contain

unreachable, nor a guaranteed available encounter. Qualify stranding, source

The sole local titan-token source is high elf 40729 in hidden camp 40772,
