## A tavern behind the chasm door

The entrance is a small alcove cut from the Underdark rock. An enormous wooden door, framed in black iron, bears an engraved tusked-ogre face. Room #88600 opens north to UnderDark chasm #844836; the chasm's south exit returns to #88600. The door east from the alcove into the tavern is closed by the zone reset. This is the only active WLD boundary, so the source places arrivals outside the tavern's main chamber until that door is opened. See the [entrance room](../../../areas/wld/morg.wld#L1) and [chasm return](../../../areas/wld/underdark.wld#L262262).

Rooms #88601–#88609 portray a crowded, shady tavern: high timbered ceilings, worn stone, tables, mugs, a large fireplace, a bar, and a hanging candle brazier. The bar room describes noisy, inebriated patrons and rats feeding on spills. The reset cast gives the rooms bouncers, trolls, drunken ogres, derro, duergar, an ogre guard, serving wenches, and a bartender. It also places Morg and his mate in the owner's quarters; Morg's description emphasizes his immense size and labored breathing, while the mate is described as much smaller and adolescent. These are authored character details, not a quest or a defined event sequence. See the [main rooms](../../../areas/wld/morg.wld#L20), [mobile cast](../../../areas/mob/morg.mob#L1), and [mobile placements](../../../areas/zon/morg.zon#L66).

The northern stone passages lead toward a back room (#88613) with half-filled mugs, marked stones, cards, and floor marks suggesting something hidden beneath the table. A reset-closed secret exit drops from that room into a moss-lit passage (#88614–#88615); the nearby rooms are a wine cellar and storeroom (#88616–#88617). A second reset-closed secret door links the cellar to five dark, filthy rooms (#88618–#88622) described as a nest of something living among bones, straw, fur, and dried blood. Resets place the wererat leader, smelly wererats, and several ugly wererats in this nest. See the [back room and passage](../../../areas/wld/morg.wld#L338), [cellar and storeroom](../../../areas/wld/morg.wld#L401), [nest rooms](../../../areas/wld/morg.wld#L435), and [wererat prototypes](../../../areas/mob/morg.mob#L210).
