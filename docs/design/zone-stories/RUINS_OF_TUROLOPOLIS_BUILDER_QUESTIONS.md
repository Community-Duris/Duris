# The Ruins of Turolopolis builder questions

Canonical key: `area:willem`. Physical coverage row 347. The
[source-comprehensive dossier](RUINS_OF_TUROLOPOLIS.md) and
[repository-wide audit supplement](../quest-story-followups/TUROLOPOLIS_AUDIT_REVIEW.md)
record the six native offerings, five badge/statue routes, foreign giver,
source history, and current access limits. These questions gather the remaining
content decisions without changing native behavior.

## Explicit builder questions and recommended resolutions

1. **Should the six native offerings remain six independent story outcomes?**
   They accept six exact item sets; only the lesser-to-greater bloodsaber branch
   connects two receipts. **Recommend** keep each accepted outcome separately
   owned, with the optional upgrade branch shown without a mandatory memorial
   history requirement.
2. **How should Lothrell's memorial wording treat the five living trapped
   heroes?** The dialogue calls them presumed dead, but all five statue targets
   are living characters. **Recommend** clarify the memorial text or design
   explicit escape endpoints before claiming a rescue occurred.
3. **Should the five badge requirements remain exact and colour-distinct?** One
   brown, green, blue, red, and black badge is accepted; white and duplicates
   do not substitute. **Recommend** retain five separate count-one checks and
   keep ordinary item names/colours visible in guidance.
4. **How should the dual use of each badge be explained?** Matching badges
   unlock statue hatches and the central passages, then the all-five memorial
   consumes them. **Recommend** warn that giving away the set can remove a
   needed access key, and distinguish possession from successful unlocking.
5. **Is RUB the intended statue activation command?** The custom travel
   portals use `CMD_RUB` (259); TOUCH is not the handler. **Recommend** preserve
   the authored verb in player guidance and record completion only after the
   selected portal successfully changes location.
6. **Should statues and return portals move the party or only the selected
   traveler?** Their type-25 portals use shared travel behavior; the reviewed
   follower path is not the special single-target follower transport.
   **Recommend** qualify group and follower behavior separately from the
   activator's route.
7. **Is local SEARCH meant to be part of discovering the statue hatches and
   other hidden routes?** Several secret/pickproof directions require a
   separate search, matching-key unlock, open, and travel sequence.
   **Recommend** keep these stages separate and do not award access for simply
   reading dialogue or trying a direction.
8. **Should Corwyck's horn exchange visibly cleanse the fountain?** The QST
   accepts unicorn horn #7103 and rewards bunker key #7100, but the source does
   not mutate the fountain or local animals. **Recommend** decide whether the
   line is metaphor or add a separately reviewed world effect before claiming
   cleansing happened.
9. **Should the minotaur's letter remain an item sourced from the current
   minotaur?** Its QST trades blue ooze and then retires him, while the same
   mobile carries time-worn letter #7135. **Recommend** preserve the conflict
   as an explicit branch decision until transfer, recovery, and recipient
   renewal are confirmed.
10. **How should players be warned that the ooze exchange can remove the
    letter source?** Retirement destroys remaining equipment and inventory.
    **Recommend** provide reviewed branch guidance or a deliberate handoff
    before retirement; do not add a fabricated QST reward or guarantee a drop.
11. **Should Surface encounter and Willem home discovery remain separate
    journal facts?** Lothrell's only active reset is in Surface room #515264,
    while the memorial belongs to zone 71. **Recommend** retain physical source
    ownership and link the encountered foreign giver to the home journal only
    after both visibility rules are honored.
12. **What should player guidance say about Lothrell's empty local start and
    one-way Surface dispersal room?** Room #7198 has six outgoing Surface
    exits but no selected current inbound WLD edge or portal; the active reset
    is foreign. **Recommend** name the actual source and qualify safe arrival
    and return rather than implying he starts in the ruins.
13. **Is the lesser bloodsaber intentionally valid without memorial history?**
    Kurtukr accepts the exact blade plus caecilia stinger #7139 to produce the
    greater blade. **Recommend** keep accepted exact inputs valid when supplied
    and avoid making the earlier memorial a historical prerequisite.
14. **How should optional preparation materials be presented?** Eleven
    material checks record current loose-item possession, not first recovery,
    personal kills, learned dialogue, or completed offerings. **Recommend**
    label them optional and avoid awarding historical proof from present
    inventory alone.
15. **Should the slave's skull offering remain limited to Willem's named
    skull?** QST #273 accepts object #7154 and coins, not an ordinary carved
    skull or proof that the other prisoners were freed. **Recommend** preserve
    the exact object and reward and avoid implying a liberation campaign.
16. **Are jail-key use and opening the four cells meant to imply an escort or
    escape?** The map provides locked cells and prisoners but no accepted
    endpoint for escort or release. **Recommend** keep opening, meeting, and
    any future rescue outcome distinct.
17. **Should secret-shop and restaurant routes remain separate from quest
    completion?** Their SEARCH/OPEN paths and return routes are map access; the
    story receipts do not prove them. **Recommend** keep access and offerings
    as separate journal facts.
18. **What travel and recovery guidance is intended for the four fall rooms?**
    Their chances are 2, 5, 10, and 90 percent, with flight/levitation, riding,
    climbing, survival, and movement conditions. **Recommend** describe the
    chances as risks, not guaranteed travel, damage, or recovery.
19. **Should six candidate daily units be presented as renewable every day?**
    Five recipients depart and cap-one materials constrain available stock.
    **Recommend** qualify active reset generation, recipient renewal, and
    accounting settlement before promising daily availability.
20. **How should nominal XP, coin, and item rewards be reported?** Shared
    recipient policy, retirement, recovery, replay, and cold publication remain
    relevant to actual delivery. **Recommend** keep authored reward values
    separate from proof that every group member received them.
