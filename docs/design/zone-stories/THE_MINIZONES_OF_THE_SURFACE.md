## Supply and progression stories

**Two hermit errands.** The Misty Chasm shaman's Q 11 accepts a small scale
15017 and a large scale 15018 for potion 15008, then retires. Both have active
salamander declarations; the small scale also has a ground declaration. The
podaling's Q 187 uses six feathers 28554, one hide 28555 and 1,000 coins for
headdress 28552; Q 199 uses three hides and 500 coins for necklace 28553. Both
mixed payments remain guarded. Birds and neberi have declared materials. Local
worn headdress 97904 and necklace 97905 have matching display names but different
identities and cannot satisfy the hermit. Supplied foreign items fit without
either maker history.

The secret recipe is an actual foreign continuation: frail man 37102 at room
37440 accepts recipe 97903 plus fine sand 37106 through `mntcastl` Q 237 for three
granular potions 37105. The water dragon wears that sand. Recipe acceptance, source
recovery and later foreign delivery are separate facts; do not duplicate the
foreign outcome as Surface credit or imply the recipe alone makes the potion.

**Gleb and the ridge hermit.** Glands come from Shipyard stonescale pyrolisks
43193, shackled vehicle pyrolisks 47109 and Alatorin bladebacks 83515/83516. Some
Shipyard glands are worn and must become loose proof. Gleb's eight-copy exchange
differs from the foreign four-gland-plus-token recipe. His potion declares level
56 fire aura, fireshield and fire ward; its name does not grant a character class.
The fish vendor at Woodseer room 16650 has salmon 16635 in produced stock. Jenna
at room 5770 carries firebreather 3003, but her produced list omits it; several
other bartender declarations exist. The ridge hermit's griffon echo contains no
mobile creation, mount grant or travel terminal. Acceptance pays coins/experience
and retires the recipient.

**Six planes, one sphere.** Ogremoch 23806/23850, Imix 25440/25450,
Yan-C-Bin 24440/24450, Menzellon 12400/12450, Olhydra 23240/23250 and Demogorgon
19704/19730 are the reviewed default sources; pairs denote mobile/room. Their
carried essences are six distinct kinds. The additional `astral_tiamat` reset
references missing mobile 19921 and must not be promised as a working alternative.
Other foreign consumers compete for these essences. The strange being issues only
one sphere and one transformable wand, then retires. Six artifact services each
consume a sphere: a single reward cannot fund all six, and repeated supply needs a
surviving recipient/reset or supplied exact spheres.

**Healing tiers and commissions.** The Incarnates 55550–55554 and treant 55246
use [death-to-object](../../../src/specs/specs.winterhaven.c#L71) to place their
