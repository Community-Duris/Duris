# Phantasmagoric Caverns builder questions and recommendations

The [Valdrak source-comprehensive dossier](VALDRAK.md) and [Checkpoint 149
audit supplement](../quest-story-followups/VALDRAK_AUDIT_REVIEW.md) document
the native item return, myconid and spider sources, shop, cave routes, and
bounded external references. These questions separate the current three-item
contract from the larger ritual and community story suggested by its prose.
They do not qualify live gameplay. See [coverage row 332](../quest-story-followups/ZONE_COVERAGE_REGISTER.csv#L332).
