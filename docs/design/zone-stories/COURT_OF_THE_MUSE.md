## Real access, shared execution and competing uses

The enchanted arch 6705 in 6719 is an unlimited **enter** item teleport to
6720. Its inscription and traveller's journal contain poetic lore, without
a bound SAY/ASK unlock procedure. The [shared item handler](../../../src/magic/spell_travel.c#L932)
checks the selected object's command and destination. A clue read or object
observation alone does not establish successful travel, safe arrival or a
learned keyword. The waterfall 6730 in 6760 uses **jump** (command 264),
not enter, to reach the still cave 6713. Numbered/name lookup and live
arrival still need qualification.

The two cave pairs are 6709 north ↔ 6710 south, keyed by traveller's journal
6719 from approach room 6718, and 6710 north ↔ 6711 south, keyed by dew 6724
from Grielda. World value six yields pickable doors; all four D resets use
six, making them closed, locked and secret. The
[world loader](../../../src/world/db.c#L1511) masks the first two door-type
bits, while [D resets](../../../src/world/db.c#L4073) set closed/locked/secret
state. **A world value of four by itself does not create a secret passage.**
The [key lookup](../../../src/cmd/actmove.c#L2856) accepts exact prototype
identity in HOLD or loose inventory without requiring ITEM_KEY. Thus the
book and dew are working native key references despite locket-shaped clues;
do not misreport them as missing keys or unusable doors.

[Search](../../../src/cmd/actobj.c#L9684) can reveal a secret entrance.
After revelation, native unlocking and ordinary opening manage reciprocal
passage; pickable cave locks also have ordinary alternatives. The cave's
far end is reachable from the waterfall/cliff side, so traversing both
book/dew gates is optional exploration, not an enforced seasonal prerequisite.
Dew consumption at Kithapa should follow any desired use of that same copy
at the gate. Neither a key possession check nor the Spring receipt proves
the gate was opened.

For the red leaf, the secret closed stump door is 6768 south ↔ 6769 north,
without a key requirement: world value five, D state five. Alternatively,
dying-stump item 6709 in 6772 uses **enter** to roots 6770; climb through
6769 to leaf source 6771. Zialh's canopy is a different location.

The seasonal portal 6700 in ledge 6797 uses **enter** to Wheel of Time 6796,
whose up exit leads to dream-tree trunk 6700. The tree route reaches the
priestess in 6704. Above her, 6704 up → 6798 is pickproof, keyed by fae key
6713, and secret/closed/locked after D state six. The reverse down exit is
pickproof, closed/locked by D state two and has key zero. The key declares
100 percent break chance. [Unlocking](../../../src/cmd/actmove.c#L3109)
manages the reciprocal lock; [key destruction](../../../src/cmd/actmove.c#L118)
must also pass durable ownership/transaction acceptance. Do not promise
destruction after a rejected settlement.

From 6798, shimmering portal 6718 uses **enter** to actual Muse court 6706,
whose ordinary down exit returns to 6704. Normal reciprocal opening supports
return after the intended entry. A crash/restart or reset restoring the
locked, key-zero reverse door creates a separate recovery episode to qualify;
it is not sufficient evidence to declare every ordinary visitor stranded.
Mode-one reset policy and held-key destruction must be tested with that
episode before selecting any return-route repair.

Tree rooms 6700/6701 declare F25, waterfall 6759 F75, and icy canopy
6786/6787 F50. [Loader checks](../../../src/world/db.c#L1337) require a valid
down exit; the [command path](../../../src/cmd/interp.c#L1898) invokes actual
falling and current movement. Brook currents are south at strength ten in
6754/6755/6757/6758, east ten in 6756, down eighty in 6759, south eighty in
6760 and south fifty in 6761. Safe passage/survival needs accepted movement
evidence and gameplay qualification; no journal material/history check grants it.
