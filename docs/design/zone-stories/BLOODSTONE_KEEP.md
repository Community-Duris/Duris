# Bloodstone Keep

Priority 16, source area `bs`, zone 740. This is a **source-comprehensive**
map with revision 1 of the [journal](../../../areas/story/bs.story.json).
It is **not gameplay-qualified**. Active economic accounting is required for
player journals, discovery, encounters, achievements and new daily eligibility.
Existing committed-receipt recovery remains available independently.

The review covers all 158 native blocks: 64 Q, one QA, 75 M and 18 MA; 65
exchanges and 74 addressable dialogue families. It also covers all 918 rooms,
255 mobile prototypes, 300 objects, 34 shop records and 1,624 reset commands
(720 M, 359 E, 311 G, 126 D, 62 O, 29 P, 17 F), including parent, chance and
live-cap semantics. The complete local special implementation, assignment order,
relevant shared execution, and bounded foreign material producers/consumers
were reviewed. The [reproducible source index](../../reference/zone-story-audits/bs.md)
retains exact native bindings and source lines; raw sources remain authoritative.

The header's actual reset mode is **2**, standard timed scheduling. It must
not be confused with difficulty or the Twin Keeps' mode-zero epic reset.
Chance and global live caps do not guarantee daily supply, recipient presence
or successful committed generation.
