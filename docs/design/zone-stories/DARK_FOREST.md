# Dark Forest

The active dark_forest area uses zone header #750 and spans rooms #75001–#75171. It defines 34 mobile and 15 object prototypes. The authored zone title is unique among the 442 current .zon sources. The active area list labels its broader range “Arthorn.” See the [active area list](../../../areas/AREA#L269), [zone header](../../../areas/zon/dark_forest.zon#L1), and [coverage row 69](../quest-story-followups/ZONE_COVERAGE_REGISTER.csv#L69).

## Environmental story

The woods are dense, thorned, and laced with dark vines. Cut trails and scattered tracks pass through several creature territories. A burnt patch and a perfectly circular ash clearing sit among the forest paths. The clearing has mystic symbols in its ash, and a nearby pit holds logs and flames that burn without being consumed; the pit’s stone ring bears the same symbols. The text does not explain who made the fire or symbols. Elsewhere, an ancient oak stands in its own open patch of sunlight, and a dead tree displays the remains of many travelers. See the [burned rooms](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L118), [symbolic clearing](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L148), [ever-burning pit](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L1070), [ancient oak](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L756), and [tree of death](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L1668).

Urd trails and an ancient hollow log give way to an Ogrillon encampment. An old battleground lies among its huts, and the hidden fortress Gar-Thurin contains a throne room and a chamber of magic that appear older than the current occupants. The forest then reaches a filthy Spriggan village of burrows and hanging carcasses. Its shaman’s burrow holds a tarot reading and feathered staff. At the River of Cu’L Torik, a room text tells an in-world legend of a Spriggan and Orc conflict with the “Black Skulls”: the river appears during the final battle and sweeps away the Orc army. The nearby statue depicts a giant Spriggan casting a spell. This is authored local mythology, not independently verified history. See the [Ogrillon battleground](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L1538), [fortress throne room](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L2349), [magic chamber](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L2217), [shaman’s burrow](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L2647), [river legend](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L2710), and [river statue](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L2816).

At the northern forest edge, the paths pass through web-covered Ettercap woods. Sticky webs are described as difficult to escape, with bones and stored prey hanging above the path. The map then descends through a chasm into a fallen island of forest and reaches a three-room swinging bridge over the River Cu’L Torik. The bridge descriptions warn that its rotten supports and swaying vines make crossing precarious. See the [webbed woods](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L3239), [chasm path](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L3401), [bridge](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L3597), and [bridge room flags](../../../areas/wld/dark_forest.wld#L3597).
