## Existing ordered kill campaign

`update_achievements` starts AIP_YOUSTRAHDME after an eligible Doru 91031 kill,
advances it to AIP_YOUSTRAHDME2 on Chernovog 58835, then changes it to
ACH_YOUSTRAHDME on Castle Strahd 58383 and invokes the existing epic reward.
Solo and qualifying nearby group experience callers apply the existing eligibility;
this is not restricted to the player who deals the last hit. Completed status
enables the Lightbringer title and existing physical/magical proc behavior.
Catacomb Strahd forms and native skull deliveries are separate outcomes.

Login removes both incomplete AIP stages, retaining completed ACH. That may be
an intentional single-session challenge rather than a persistence bug. Builders
must choose retained attempt boundaries versus a durable campaign before changing
cleanup. The current zone journal does not consume these tags. An adapter needs
exact victim generation, outcome identity, frozen eligible group recipients,
ordered stage/attempt rules and recoverable achievement/reward publication.
Donated hearts or skulls must never fabricate this personal combat history.
