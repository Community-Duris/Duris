terrain into new zone records. A future dynamic journal can filter encountered
contacts by nearby region, show independent requests and competing consumers,
display `Ready now` for current materials and saved receipts for accepted
deliveries, and reveal access/campaign detail as contacts are encountered.

identifiers as player commands. Read-only hints never mutate acquisition/history.

and repeated-count preparation; supplied versus personally recovered proof;
four separate heart allocations, competing recipient retirement and two consumed
eggs; independent opposing receipts; support exclusion; actual connector/rift/
teleport arrival; source/import/reset availability; and receipt reload/replay.
Mixed fees, first fishing/death-created source, actor/world transformations and
full campaign policy remain capability work. Focused offline fixtures can prove
catalog, display and receipt semantics, without certifying played combat,
source, paid, travel or campaign journeys.
