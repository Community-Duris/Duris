# The Goblin Camp

This source dossier covers the active Goblin Camp contributor: 21 rooms (#11400–#11420), nine mobile prototypes, and four object prototypes. The route, reset, dependency, title, and history review is in the [Goblin Camp audit supplement](../quest-story-followups/GOBLINCAMP_AUDIT_REVIEW.md).

## A surface camp cut from the forest

The camp sits in a once-peaceful forest. Its entrance is filthy, the ground is trampled, and the goblins have slaughtered local animals for food. Inside, brush and trees are cut for fires, limbs and bark have been stripped, and timber has been stacked or left in disorder. The largest bonfire burns at the camp’s center, and a wall made from trees encloses much of the settlement ([entrance](../../../areas/wld/goblincamp.wld#L1), [center](../../../areas/wld/goblincamp.wld#L325), [timber clearing](../../../areas/wld/goblincamp.wld#L473)).

Waste is part of the landscape. Room #11403 describes a garbage pit full of trash and animal remains, rats and larvae, dead plants, and water the narrator believes may be permanently poisoned. The interior also contains fire pits, bones, bedrolls, damaged trees, and muddy paths ([dump](../../../areas/wld/goblincamp.wld#L83), [dump and bone resets](../../../areas/zon/goblincamp.zon#L9)).

## A timber party that stayed

Room text says the goblins came from the Underdark city of Malch’hor Ganl to gather timber for their homeland. After arriving, they found themselves on their own and built a perimeter wall. The text says they now appear likely to stay, though another room wonders whether they came from a botched raid or a lumber mission ([room #11415](../../../areas/wld/goblincamp.wld#L410), [room #11416](../../../areas/wld/goblincamp.wld#L441)).

The camp is a community of warriors, guards, women, children, and elders. A toddler has never seen the goblin homeland; an elderly man fears the younger warriors, and an elderly woman has become senile. The shaman is described as an important figurehead who has lost much of his mind and no longer leads in practice ([child](../../../areas/mob/goblincamp.mob#L48), [elder](../../../areas/mob/goblincamp.mob#L63), [shaman](../../../areas/mob/goblincamp.mob#L106)). One description says older goblins feed on and kill forest animals that come near; another says the guards gamble more than they keep watch ([north wall](../../../areas/wld/goblincamp.wld#L300), [wooden wall](../../../areas/wld/goblincamp.wld#L501)).

The objects support the camp’s daily life and violence: the dump holds a goblin thigh bone used as a crude weapon, warriors carry iron swords, and the shaman wears a pinewood totem described as glowing with power ([bone](../../../areas/obj/goblincamp.obj#L14), [totem](../../../areas/obj/goblincamp.obj#L27), [sword](../../../areas/obj/goblincamp.obj#L44), [equipment resets](../../../areas/zon/goblincamp.zon#L12)).

## Source boundary

Both the full area roster and QST roster list Goblin Camp. The local QST source and SHP file are absent, and there is no story sidecar or native Q/QA contract. This dossier describes checked-in room, mobile, object, and reset text; it does not establish the goblins’ current population, their future plans, or live gameplay.
