# Golden Hall of the Crown: comprehensive source story map

**Source-comprehensive, revision one — October 3, 2026. Gameplay qualification
remains open.** The [zone sidecar](../../../areas/story/gold_hal.story.json)
classifies every native exchange into nine independent outcomes and seven
support services, with 27 contacts/all seventeen addressed families and 34
optional preparation/history checks. Nine outcomes count for zone progress;
seven are potential daily candidates. The two experience-awarding rescues
that return their offered key remain visible and achievement eligible, while
their existing native `Item exchange` daily exclusions remain intact.

Active, ready economic accounting is mandatory for player discovery,
encounters, journals and new credit. A source declaration, current item check,
dialogue suggestion or optional producer receipt does not prove personal
recovery, combat, travel or a complete campaign. This checkpoint ships journal
guidance and documentation; **no native zone or quest repair is implemented.**

## Shared capability and accounting gaps

The existing native offer continuation records accepted exact item sets,
outputs and recipient retirement. Keep its accounting/reward recovery ordering.
All three mixed-fee services remain guarded until items, wallet debit, outputs,
recipient episode and receipt commit together; guidance and synthetic receipts
do not enable their runtime path. Ordinary plant-golem/Flakun purchases,
world quests and [explicit inn rental](../../../src/specs/specs.room.c#L243)
retain separate service rules. The inn assignment works without requiring a
ROOM_INN flag in the handler; absence of that flag alone is not a missing inn.

Tield alone has ACT_TEACHER. The master knight/priestess have teacher names
and training prose, but lack that flag and another found local teaching binding.
[FindTeacher](../../../src/guild/guild.c#L410) requires the flag for ordinary
NPC teaching. Builder should choose instructional roles or flavor titles;
their valid quest exchange/source roles remain intact either way. Paid lessons
need accepted lesson/state evidence before journal stages can assert learning.

The [Reliance procedure](../../../src/specs/specs.jot.c#L596) requires the sword
worn, a say argument containing `reliance`, no current ride/fight, prime-plane
location and room size/locker admission. After the item timer allows a call,
it searches any player link for mobile 40429: an already-ridden mount refuses;
an existing unmounted one may move to the player and impose wait. Only fresh
creation updates the item timer. Thus the 200-second fresh-creation timer is
not a universal cooldown between existing-mount relocations. The drain message
has no separate resource debit in this function. Fresh creation uses
`setup_pet(..., -1, PET_NOCASH)`, follower/charm and mount flags; bounded pet
setup removes cash and experience. A linked/created mount is not a qualified
durable summon, recovery or source achievement.

Expand the shared plan with accepted source-versus-player-gift provenance,
exact container/source incarnation, output UID lineage (including returned
same-kind keys), retirement and current custody. Record confirmed switch,
door, inscription and travel outcomes separately from suggested commands.
If builders want combat proofs, persist qualifying death/group attribution
and an attempt policy instead of treating a predeclared head/totem as a kill.
Only accepted actor-state/arrival events can establish a rescue beyond native
retirement. Qualify Reliance's create-versus-relocate result, link type/owner,
pet recovery, item timer and interruption before a summon milestone.

Keep the two rescues' daily exclusions until an explicit policy distinguishes
meaningful experience/retirement outcomes from pure same-item returns, with
recipient episode/reset and abuse/recovery evidence. Their zone achievements
already count; correcting a daily policy is not a missing rescue implementation.
A builder-selected full royal investigation family would need required accepted
stages, alternative supplied-proof policy and durable episode/state ownership.
Current terminal receipts do not establish that larger campaign.
