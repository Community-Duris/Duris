# The Tunnels of Cyric the Destroyer

The active cyrictunnel area uses zone header #545, covers rooms #54500–#54581, and defines 16 mobile and 10 object prototypes. Its authored zone title is unique among the 442 current .zon sources. The area list's legacy range note is *545. See the [active area list](../../../areas/AREA#L232), [zone header](../../../areas/zon/cyrictunnel.zon#L1), and [coverage row 68](../quest-story-followups/ZONE_COVERAGE_REGISTER.csv#L68).

## Environmental story

The upper route begins in the Mountains of Cyric. The entry description spells the name as Cryic, while the surrounding Surface sources spell it Cyric. A winding mountain tunnel grows warmer and airless as it descends. A rubble cave-in divides the upper corridor from the lower black tunnels, where groans, snarls, and heat lead toward a cavernous lava lake.

The lava cavern's center holds a diorite statue. Its object description recounts an in-world legend: Cyric loses control on the Day of BloodLust in year 87, a meteor shower reshapes the southern lands of Duris, and a crater and lava flow burn a realm. The same text says the fallen realm later rises under a similar violent influence. Treat this as the zone's authored legend; the source does not independently establish its chronology. See the [statue prototype](../../../areas/obj/cyrictunnel.obj#L25) and the [lava-lake room text](../../../areas/wld/cyrictunnel.wld#L283).

The lower passages turn from a natural cavern into a cult complex. Black tunnels carry demonic and Cyric runes; a goat skull marks an intersection; a torture chamber holds flesh hooks and blood. The fanatical necromancer says he sacrifices children and lost adventurers, then raises their bodies as undead. Worshipers carve Cyric's symbol into their faces, an entranced follower kneels on human-skin pillows, and the high priest blesses the followers with sacramental blood. A small imp prepares the dagger. Zombies, skeletons, spectres, flaming skeletons, bats, and tormented spirits fill the surrounding passages. The room descriptions and [mobile prototypes](../../../areas/mob/cyrictunnel.mob#L61) supply the full sequence.

## Quest and interaction boundary

There is no local cyrictunnel.qst, story sidecar, or SHP file. The historical [QST area index](../../../areas/QST/AREA#L212) lists cyrictunnel, but that index is not the production quest list and does not create a local quest contract. No local Q/QA completion contract is defined.

The reset data and objects do define a key-and-switch puzzle. A zombie sentry at the diorite door carries the small diorite key #54503; that key matches the locked south exit from room #54535. The necromancer carries runed key #54504; it matches the locked floor exit beneath the statue at #54519. The statue is an ITEM_SWITCH attached to that floor exit. Two rubble objects are also ITEM_SWITCH objects, one on each side of the cave-in. Their push targets match the two exits the reset marks blocked. The shared item_switch handler clears the selected blocked flag and, for a non-secret exit, its reverse as well. These mechanics explain the strict reset graph; they have not been exercised in a live game. See the [reset records](../../../areas/zon/cyrictunnel.zon#L8), [rubble switches](../../../areas/obj/cyrictunnel.obj#L1), [statue switch](../../../areas/obj/cyrictunnel.obj#L25), [generic item switch](../../../src/specs/specs.object.c#L302), and [object switch binding](../../../src/world/db.c#L5281).

The 14 rooms describing the lava lake and flows use sector value 11, SECT_FIREPLANE. The source defines lava separately as sector 39, and movement code invokes firesector() when entering either FIREPLANE or LAVA rooms. This is a material environment assignment with a live effect, though no gameplay hazard or safe route was tested. See [sector constants](../../../src/core/defines.h#L469) and [movement handling](../../../src/world/handler.c#L1542).

Active WLD links connect the upper opening to Surface, three underdark ledges or fissures to the Underdark, and the small rocky hole to Connector Zones. An inactive surf.wld map supplies five additional source-only approaches to four of those same local boundaries. The audit supplement records each route and its limits.
