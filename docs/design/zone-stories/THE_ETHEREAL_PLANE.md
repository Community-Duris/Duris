# The Ethereal Plane

This source dossier describes the active contributor listed as `ethereal_main` in `areas/AREA`. The zone covers rooms #12401-#12550 and contains 150 rooms, eight mobiles, and 32 objects. The source-based route and dependency review is in the [Ethereal Main audit supplement](../quest-story-followups/ETHEREAL_MAIN_AUDIT_REVIEW.md).

## The plane in the room text

Most rooms share the title “The Ethereal Plane.” Their descriptions make the place feel light, gray, and without firm boundaries: debris hangs weightless, and shapes from other planes fade in and out of view. The text builds an open, unsettled dimension rather than a settlement with named districts.

Two rooms break that pattern. Room #12494, “A Void in Time,” is described as a lightless pocket without dust or movement, a vacuum of time and space. Room #12511, “A Pocket of Antimatter,” fills the scene with lightning-like sparks, dark clouds, a fierce solar wind, and distortion of time, color, and space. These rooms carry the zone’s strongest environmental shifts ([room #12494](../../../areas/wld/ethereal_main.wld#L3907), [room #12511](../../../areas/wld/ethereal_main.wld#L4617)).

The plane opens into two named neighboring spaces. From room #12442 the authored downward exit continues toward the Dream; from #12480 a westward exit reaches Dreggan Woods. Both have reciprocal exits back into the Ethereal Plane. The authored directions frame the area as a crossing place between unusual realms ([room #12442](../../../areas/wld/ethereal_main.wld#L1723), [room #12480](../../../areas/wld/ethereal_main.wld#L3319)).

## Its inhabitants and objects

The reset roster places nightmares, shadow daemons, ethereal daemons, smoke and mist mephits, and grey thought-eaters through the plane. Menzellon is the named anchor encounter. His compiled shout behavior can call “Denizens of the Ether” to pursue an enemy, using local Ethereal mobiles as helpers ([Menzellon behavior](../../../src/specs/specs.elemental_bosses.c#L15), [binding](../../../src/specs/specs.assign.c#L1217)).

The object set includes a cloak of ethereal mists, the faded-red dagger Silencer, a black eyepiece, rings, masks, and several rifts or pools. The local zone resets the cloak and Silencer on Menzellon. Heavens’ QST source refers to those two local objects as ethereal components for its Rozkadrath stage ([local resets](../../../areas/zon/ethereal_main.zon#L56), [Heavens QST references](../../../areas/qst/heavens.qst#L85)).

The rifts give the Ethereal Plane its source-defined links to other realms. Reset-loaded objects lead toward the Astral and elemental planes, Celestia, and the Fortress of Dreams. Other active areas reset pools or portals that target Ethereal rooms. The audit supplement maps these records separately from ordinary WLD exits because object presence, command use, and live access are not established by static source data.

## Source boundary

The full-world roster includes `ethereal_main` and the QST roster also lists it, while the local QST file is absent. No local SHP file or story sidecar is present. The notes here describe checked-in source and compiled behavior; they do not claim current in-game population, portal availability, or player traversal.
