Reviewed October 2, 2026. Source area `caertannad`, zone 784, journal revision 1.
The review covers all 451 rooms, 123 mobiles, 121 objects, 1,614 resets and
116 native blocks: six Q, 24 QA, 77 M and nine MA. There is no local shop file.

service, with 24 contacts, all 37 addressable topic families and fifteen optional
producer checks. This is source-comprehensive; active gameplay remains unqualified.

## Availability, source alternatives and ownership

Local recipient mobiles have cap one. Thirteen contracts remove their giver.
In particular, Hindis's head reward removes the figurine recipient, and
CrowFoot's message reward removes the egg-bounty recipient. Their other
exchanges keep them present. These are encounter-lifetime constraints, not an
encoded exclusive branch. Brothedin also leaves despite repeat-supply wording.

The zone header's reset mode is **zero**, not its final difficulty value two.
Normal boot still invokes reset generation; ordinary timed reset scheduling
requires a nonzero mode. Committed epic touches request reset events for mode-zero
zones, and the reviewed `no_reset_zone_reset` path checks epic completion and
database reset percentage. Recovery/copyover can preserve existing episodes.
Do not promise a fresh giver every day or rewrite the mode without a content
decision. Thirteen disappearing contracts are story-only; sixteen other
achievement contracts are potential daily units, still subject to active
accounting, supported offerings and telemetry. Mungir adds no daily unit.

Kyzastaxkasis has a 30% M chance and cap one; scales have a separate 10% G chance
and cap one. The concentrated void elemental at 78526 has a 16% M chance and
cap one; its crystal has 100% G and cap one. The shared M admission allows
chance-loaded mobiles through the reviewed force-repopulation path, rather
than retrying those chance entries on every ordinary reset. Rarity, reset
admission, stock cap and actual reachable location all need played evidence.
The high-drop list's Kyzastaxkasis branch is commented out; it adds no extra
active scales producer.

Warlords begin in Alteran dispersion room 78538 and may wander onto foreign
regional paths; each heart has cap one. Hearts are existing inventory. Fire

sources; each harpy tribe's feathers cap six; ravasaur teeth cap fourteen;
FrostSharn scimitars cap six; claws cap 41; bandanas cap twelve; plagued skulls
cap twenty. Numerous region populations begin in dispersion rooms, so initial
reset room and current encounter location must not be conflated.

Silverleaf has a 65% O source, cap one, at Tharnadia Rifts 116025, and two 60%
sources, cap two, at Elemental Plane 3807/3808. The rope-bridge tree description
does not create a separate local leaf. Basilisk egg is 100% O stock at 78754,
cap one, despite rarity prose. There is no reviewed local hatching/expiry

Alatorin owns Sturb's single-wing token/250000-XP exchange; Kirrb and Doklin's
