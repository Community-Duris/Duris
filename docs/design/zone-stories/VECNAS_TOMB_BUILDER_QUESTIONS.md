# Vecna's Tomb builder questions and recommendations

The [source-comprehensive dossier](VECNAS_TOMB.md) and [Checkpoint 143
audit supplement](../quest-story-followups/VECNAS_TOMB_AUDIT_REVIEW.md) map two
independent native item returns, the tomb's scripted encounters, and its active
and inactive route records. This checklist makes twenty direct-source builder
decisions explicit. It does not add contracts, authorize source repairs, or
certify played results. See [coverage row 336](../quest-story-followups/ZONE_COVERAGE_REGISTER.csv#L336).

scripted travel, item custody, combat, rewards, and renewal evidence distinct.
They do not certify current giver stock, route admission, item availability,
fall outcomes, reward delivery, or reset execution.
