# Dragonnia

Active `dragonnia` is zone #68, titled “Dragonnia,” with 67 rooms (#6800–#6869), 32 mobile prototypes, and 67 object prototypes. It appears in both `areas/AREA` and `areas/qst/AREA`, but has no local QST, shop file, or story sidecar. Its zone contains 173 non-`S` reset commands, including 20 door states, 8 room-object loads, 10 container loads, 73 mobile loads, 49 equipment loads, and 13 gifts. The header uses reset mode 0. See the [area list](../../../areas/AREA#L37), [zone header and resets](../../../areas/zon/dragonnia.zon#L1), [QST area index](../../../areas/qst/AREA#L34), [rooms](../../../areas/wld/dragonnia.wld#L1), [mobile prototypes](../../../areas/mob/dragonnia.mob#L1), [object prototypes](../../../areas/obj/dragonnia.obj#L1), and [coverage row 79](../quest-story-followups/ZONE_COVERAGE_REGISTER.csv#L79).

## A one-way, dangerous entrance

The city is reached through a slick shaft descending from a hidden chamber in the chasm wall. Its room text warns that moss and slop leave no refuge from a long fall; the four fall fields are 80% at #6800 and 100% at #6860–#6862. At the base, #6804 says the hole is too high and slippery to climb. The source also resets a DemoDragon (#6801) in the outside chamber, and its special blocks the `down` command there. See the [shaft and outside chamber](../../../areas/wld/dragonnia.wld#L1), [fall fields](../../../areas/wld/dragonnia.wld#L1052), [DemoDragon reset](../../../areas/zon/dragonnia.zon#L108), and [movement handler](../../../src/specs/specs.dragonnia.c#L310).

## The dragon brotherhoods and their empty city

The city rooms describe main roads once walked by thousands of dragons, largely abandoned halls, bones, bloodied chambers, and five color-based brotherhood houses: black, white, blue, green, and red. Each has a waiting room where young dragons were tested by a master. Their halls and mobile descriptions name distinct breath attacks and initiation ranks. The source holds both past-tense signs of abandonment and a reset population of guards, brotherhood masters, clergy, and dragons; it does not explain the chronology.

The golden dragon quarter says its owner is the last of his kind, which the empress’s hordes obliterated. The golden dragon’s mobile repeats that account and is reset in the city. At the far end of the palace, Dragonnia is a five-headed empress described as having conquered and killed dragons who resisted her. Her death has a source-coded consequence: the mobile handler creates the beating heart #55082, which decays after roughly three MUD days. Active Winterhaven and Alatorin quests both consume that heart alongside the hearts of Tiamat and Bahamut. See the [golden quarter](../../../areas/wld/dragonnia.wld#L370), [Empress reset](../../../areas/zon/dragonnia.zon#L204), [heart prototype](../../../areas/obj/wh.obj#L804), [death handler](../../../src/specs/specs.winterhaven.c#L4669), and the [three-heart exchange](../../../areas/qst/wh.qst#L3066).

## The palace, Teviros, and the dragon masters

The palace’s carved causeway leads through two exterior gates and a grand hall to the royal chamber. Multiple doorways reset closed and locked; several are keyed to local objects carried by dragons or nested in the golden treasure chest. The WLD key field on the palace-hall pair between #6834 and #6835 is asymmetric: one side names key #6863 and the other #6864. The zone also resets a black-blue key (#6866) that no WLD door uses. See the [palace doors](../../../areas/wld/dragonnia.wld#L624), [door resets](../../../areas/zon/dragonnia.zon#L8), [key objects](../../../areas/obj/dragonnia.obj#L994), and [chest contents](../../../areas/zon/dragonnia.zon#L95).

The city’s temple venerates Teviros as its dragon god. The source describes living stone, divine battle images, a sacred sanctum with objects donated by the god, a monastery, and a guarded clergy hierarchy. A custom sanctum room handler changes `unlock` behavior based on whether the archbishop or bishop carries their keys; the “key to the holy sanctum” can melt and injure its holder, while the bishop’s key can block unlocking. The source does not explain the intended resolution. See the [Temple of Teviros](../../../areas/wld/dragonnia.wld#L763), [sanctum handler](../../../src/specs/specs.dragonnia.c#L398), and [key resets](../../../areas/zon/dragonnia.zon#L236).

A hot statue room incubates dragon eggs. The main treasure room contains nested gold, jewels, and a white-and-blue key. Other items include dragon-scale armor, the mystic axe of the dragonkind, and a stone called the totem of the ancient dragon riders. The axe is reset on the empress and has a compiled “protect me” effect. The totem’s inscription says “LIFE”; its compiled effect heals its wearer and can return them to a room after the wearer says “ilienze,” but no local reset places it. See the [egg chamber](../../../areas/wld/dragonnia.wld#L662), [treasure reset](../../../areas/zon/dragonnia.zon#L95), [dragonkind handler](../../../src/specs/specs.underworld.c#L188), [totem](../../../areas/obj/dragonnia.obj#L300), and [totem handler](../../../src/specs/specs.dragonnia.c#L1328).

## Dynamic arrivals and unfinished source details

An equipped glowing stone (#6860) is a local `ITEM_TELEPORT` targeting the egg chamber #6836; it is reset on a dragon statue in the temple with a 10% load chance. This is a source-defined target rather than a WLD edge. No external teleport or switch object targets the local rooms.

The local zone also loads Heavens objects #360, #1210, and #910. Three `G #4050` reset commands have no matching object prototype in the current object sources. Several mobile and object prototypes are not referenced by local resets, including a steel sentinel dragon described as raised by Keyvan, the golden dragon statue, an ancient dragon-rider totem, and a black-blue key. See the [external object resets](../../../areas/zon/dragonnia.zon#L89), [Heavens objects](../../../areas/obj/heavens.obj#L3725), [teleport object](../../../areas/obj/dragonnia.obj#L954), and [reset-use inventory](../quest-story-followups/DRAGONNIA_AUDIT_REVIEW.md#typed-dependencies-and-source-only-references).
