# Die Hall (bastard source)

Canonical key: `component:bastard`. Physical coverage row 364. The raw ZON
title is “Die hall”; the source stem is `bastard`. Neither appears in the full
area or QST area lists, and the source has no quest or story sidecar. The
[technical audit](../quest-story-followups/BASTARD_AUDIT_REVIEW.md) records
its numeric room overlap with active Underworld3 and source-only Sunhall.

## A guarded root and a dug-out cavern

The map begins beside an exposed root of Yggdrasil under the Giants Run
Mountains. Frost giants guard the passage into a large durithalite cavern,
which its description attributes to a duergar mining company. A carved
signature mark is attributed to Clavados. The cave text describes carved
tunnels, preserved corpses, dark torches, and a staircase toward a raiding
guild's sleeping and gathering places.

The west alcove is described as Ilixctthu's forbidden guild shop. Its text
leaves open whether the illithid was enslaved or supports the guild freely.
Beyond the cave, the Blood and Skull Tavern separates lodging, social, and
barracks rooms. An ogre-fur bed inscription names Ungulat. The local badge
object separately calls its red skull-and-crossbones symbol the emblem of
Carnage. Preserve the Clavados and Carnage references as separate source text
until the builder identifies their relationship.
