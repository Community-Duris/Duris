# Guildhalls

This dossier covers active contributor guildhalls, the shared room and object templates used by the persistent guildhall feature. The [repository-wide audit supplement](../quest-story-followups/GUILDHALLS_AUDIT_REVIEW.md) records its source census, static and code-created connectivity, typed dependencies, history, and retention decision.

## A shared hall assembled from templates

The source has 1,000 room prototypes, #48000–#48999. Fifteen have authored names. The remaining 985 are named Unnamed and have no long description. The named rooms frame a guild’s adaptable interior: a high-arched foyer before the Hall of a realm name, an antechamber buzzing with energy, an Apex of Knowledge, a Peaceful Escape, a chamber of dancing lights and mirrors, sleeping quarters, an alcove, a room inside a looking glass, a vaulted chamber, a portal room, and a room of nautical charts. The foyer’s %s is filled with the guild name by the runtime room initializer when no custom name is saved ([room templates](../../../areas/wld/guildhalls.wld#L1), [name substitution](../../../src/guild/guildhall_rooms.c#L103)).

Four named rooms carry full environmental descriptions. The Forge describes a fieldstone hearth and kingdom ore waiting to become weapons and mail. The Weaving Hall turns field fibre into cloth and leather. The Jeweller’s Workshop lays out cut stones and precious metals. The Guild Store presents those products beside a ledger of the realm’s ore, timber, fibre, and water ([Forge](../../../areas/wld/guildhalls.wld#L56), [Weaving Hall](../../../areas/wld/guildhalls.wld#L68), [Jeweller’s Workshop](../../../areas/wld/guildhalls.wld#L79), [Guild Store](../../../areas/wld/guildhalls.wld#L90)). These newer descriptions make the economic life of a realm the clearest sustained environmental story in the area. The other named templates mostly set a mood or a room purpose through their short name.

Three oversized golem statues—djinn, Anubian warrior, and bronze cleric—stand as hall guardians in the mobile source. Their text gives them mystical energy and watchful poses, but it does not author a conflict or quest ([golem prototypes](../../../areas/mob/guildhalls.mob#L1), [warrior](../../../areas/mob/guildhalls.mob#L16), [cleric](../../../areas/mob/guildhalls.mob#L29)). The objects reinforce the shared-service setting: the hall door and portal, heartstone and fountains, storage cabinet and looking glass, bank counter, town portal, library tome, cargo board, workshop props, guild-store counter, kingdom-craft blank, and guild bulletin-board prototypes ([object catalog](../../../areas/obj/guildhalls.obj#L1), [heartstone and services](../../../areas/obj/guildhalls.obj#L17), [library and cargo](../../../areas/obj/guildhalls.obj#L97), [workshop props](../../../areas/obj/guildhalls.obj#L113), [craft blank](../../../areas/obj/guildhalls.obj#L145), [guild boards](../../../areas/obj/guildhalls.obj#L153)).

## Quest and story boundary

No local QST source, SHP file, story sidecar, or native Q/QA contract is present. The QST roster lists the contributor, but that entry does not supply a player objective or outcome. The authored ZON has reset mode 0 and two object-reset commands: heartstone object #48002 in template room #48001, and portal object #48001 in template room #48004 ([ZON header and resets](../../../areas/zon/guildhalls.zon#L1), [QST roster](../../../areas/qst/AREA#L199)). These records do not establish what a live saved hall contains.

The area’s larger narrative is the player-built realm itself: a named guildhall, a heartstone, rooms for rest and knowledge, windows and travel services, and—since the 2026 workshop update—dedicated places to make and trade kingdom goods. That system is implemented by Guildhall and kingdom code. It is not represented as a local NPC quest, and the source does not describe a particular guild’s history or a universal hall-building story.

## Builder questions and recommended resolutions

| Question for the area owner | Source evidence | Recommended resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Should the themed names and energy, mirror, heartstone, and knowledge imagery imply one shared magical story? | The foyer, antechamber, knowledge room, healing room, portal room, and looking-glass room have suggestive names, while most carry no authored description or quest result ([room templates](../../../areas/wld/guildhalls.wld#L1), [runtime template mapping](../../../src/guild/guildhall.h#L62)). | Keep these as reusable atmosphere and service names unless an owner supplies a specific guild-neutral story and outcome. Do not infer a quest from the heartstone or portal imagery. |
| Are all 985 Unnamed prototypes intentional room-pool entries, and which slots are reserved? | The WLD contains 985 blank-name rooms; the allocator starts at #48020 and tests values below #48999, while the zone contains room templates #48000–#48014 ([room pool](../../../areas/wld/guildhalls.wld#L121), [allocator](../../../src/guild/guildhall_db.c#L227)). | Preserve the blank prototypes as system data. Confirm whether #48015–#48019 and #48999 are intentional reserves; the allocator’s exclusive end and the inclusive IN_GH_ZONE bound currently differ. |
| Is the portal room meant to connect a saved hall to its outposts? | ZON places portal object #48001 in template room #48004, but PortalRoom::init sets the room pointer and retains a TODO to initialize portals to outposts ([reset](../../../areas/zon/guildhalls.zon#L10), [portal-room initializer](../../../src/guild/guildhall_rooms.c#L315)). | Treat that route as unresolved in documentation. Keep the template and reset intact; do not promise an outpost route or add an exit based on the room title. |
| Is the imported magical tome in Brass an intentional shared object? | Active Brass resets Guildhalls object #48012 in its Study Room; the older Brass candidate has a matching source-only reset ([active Brass reset](../../../areas/zon/brass.zon#L458), [tome prototype](../../../areas/obj/guildhalls.obj#L97)). | Preserve object #48012 and both source references. Any future item move needs to account for the active Brass reset and separately review the inactive Brass candidate. |
| How should guildhalls relate to the older Adventurers Guildhalls / player-castles source? | Active player_castles keeps that similar title, while current code labels legacy house and guildhall handlers deprecated; Git history explicitly renamed castle_rooms to guildhalls and records only a few copied objects from player_castles ([older title](../../../areas/zon/player_castles.zon#L2), [legacy code marker](../../../src/core/files.c#L5161)). | Keep the active sources separate. Record the shared feature lineage without calling it a complete zone replacement. Ask the respective owners before reconciling their stories or retiring either contributor. |
| Are the four kingdom workshop descriptions and the store’s service intent canonical? | The 2026 area update added four room descriptions and matching props; code wires the room types to the kingdom workshop and guild-store systems ([workshop rooms](../../../areas/wld/guildhalls.wld#L56), [room initializer](../../../src/guild/guildhall_rooms.c#L479), [kingdom craft](../../../src/kingdom/kingdom_craft.c#L615)). | Keep the descriptions with their room templates and the trade narrative tied to those services. Confirm public access, product, and realm-economy expectations in the feature owner’s documentation rather than inventing a local quest. |

The recommendations preserve the recorded service setting and distinguish its room templates from the live, persistent guildhall feature. No live guildhall records were inspected, and this dossier makes no claim about a currently instantiated hall or its player-visible route.
