### Reward use and shared support effects

The [whirlwind bracer](../../../src/specs/specs.cosmic.c#L15) must be worn on a
supported wrist. Tapping selects the actual equipped object; absent HUM charge
returns no-op text. Accepted tap consumes the charge, stores time and applies
SKILL_WHIRLWIND for three violence pulses. Periodic recharge uses the property
`timer.proc.bracerOfWhirlwinds`, default 300 seconds; removing it clears HUM.
The seal's gear grant, present possession, wearing, readiness and accepted buff
are distinct events. Extend accepted effect/cooldown publication with object UID,
wearer, exact revision and selected time, excluding denied/no-op uses and duplicate
recovery. Keep this optional combat use separate from the seal achievement.

The council room also has foreign magical fountain 72 and epic stone 359.
[Spell pool](../../../src/specs/specs.heavens.c#L963) chooses a spell at setup and
changes its selection after forty minutes; DRINK calls the selected spell and
returns true without a per-effect acceptance result. Track actual admitted
actor/effect/selection if builders want a blessing objective. Command dispatch
alone does not establish that a buff was granted. The epic stone already uses
[transactional zone touch](../../../src/world/epic.c#L1111), distinct from these
quest receipts and the four-soul seal. Its group/level/peaceful/cooldown/source
rules and committed zone awards remain authoritative.

Grudat stocks wards 73/77 with shared trap procedures, disguise kit 500 and
lockpicks 412. Hidden ward arming/owner/expiry, trigger/disarm/effect/destruction
need their own accepted evidence if authored as objectives. Local mine 193 and
ore carried by rock crunchers are mining/support content, not additional native
quests. Shops sell ordinary foundry gear and black-market supplies. Prayers,
conspiracy, pilgrimage, magical defense, council rebirth, the dying-prisoner
containers 76054 and a generic zero-value potion 76062 have no further reviewed
accepted quest endpoints. The potion is not reset/quest stock in this review;
unused data alone is not an active failure. Imprisoned unicorn 76074 has a
prototype but no local M/F reset declaration. Its captivity lore therefore
does not establish a loaded rescue quest; confirm whether it is retained flavor,
an intentionally unused creature or missing intended placement before repair.
