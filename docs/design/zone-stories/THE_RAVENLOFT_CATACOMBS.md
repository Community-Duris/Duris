# The Ravenloft Catacombs: comprehensive source story map

Priority 21, source area `ravenloft2`, canonical zone 590. This is a comprehensive
source map, not a claim of a played, accounting-active journey. Player discovery,
encounters, journal progress, achievements and new daily eligibility require
active, ready economic accounting. No accounting activation or operational world
repair is part of this mapping.

The [revision 1 journal](../../../areas/story/ravenloft2.story.json) maps all 37
native exchanges into twelve stories, thirteen requests and eight support
services: 25 achievement units, eighteen potential daily candidates, 25 contacts and fourteen
optional preparation checks. Blinsky's five accepted artifacts form one recovery
family. Each of the three skull recipients retains its own reward and receipt;
one delivery does not complete the other two. Services award no achievements.
Mode-zero disappearing contacts remain story-only. Repeatable story/request
deliveries can be daily candidates; stock and recipient availability still need
qualification. Services remain excluded even if their native exchanges repeat.

## Evidence and review boundary

Reviewed the complete active local source: 199 native blocks (35 QA, two Q,
117 MA and 45 M), comprising 37 exchanges, 135 addressed response blocks and
27 ambient responses; 400 rooms, 98 mobile and 327 object prototypes; both shops;
1,457 reset commands and all 665 grouped reset families. Reset counts are 330 M,
284 E, 247 O, 199 F, 194 D, 136 G and 67 P. The
[reproducible review index](../../reference/zone-story-audits/ravenloft2.md)
provides exact contract identities, source lines, prototypes and reset evidence.

There are no local literal special assignments or local data procedure bindings
in the reviewed source. Shared computed shopkeeper, switch and teleport roles
still apply. Reviewed their dispatch and execution, native Q/M selection,
container population, key use, speech doors, shop existing-stock behavior,
temporary vampire spell effects and accounting refusal/recovery boundaries.
No local Strahd death-to-form transformation implementation was found. The four
forms are separate reset placements, not an inferred chain of spawn events.

Bounded foreign review covers the required dracolich skull, Madam Eva crystal
ball and old catacomb key; their reset sources and relevant castle/forest rooms;
foreign holy buckler, enchantment scroll and equipment-chest reward prototypes;
the Dayheart prototype/placement; ordinary incoming cellar/Kingsmen routes;
paired handprints; and the spectral corridor's Barovia gateway continuations.
No foreign native Q contract using the local Ravenloft item range was found.
This does not qualify every foreign area's quests or source-generation journey.

## Support exchanges, dialogue and stock

Four fortune services consume one physical coin and award 1,000,000 copper and
up to 200,000 experience plus four lichbane potions from Thredra, four moonbeam
drops from Kasimir, or four Exceptional Duris equipment chests from Izek/Cyrus.
Only Cyrus retires after this exchange. Equal Izek/Cyrus payouts retain separate
recipient identities and availability. These chests are not potion outputs.

Ezmerelda's reading costs 10,000 copper and Izek's dues cost 50,000 copper as
wallet offerings. Both remain unavailable under active accounting; no wallet
readiness step pretends otherwise. The reading's raven/moon/mists choice and
twelve card replies are stateless conversational loops. A player can ask card
keywords without proving payment, a selected branch or accepted earlier topics.
Izek's promised first-light breach does not mutate a gate or grant entry.

The image of Rahadin 59090 exchanges old key 58419 for a replacement of that
kind plus spectral key 59288, then retires. Jander exchanges raw ant pincer
59201 for three seared ant meats 59323. Both remain visible services without
achievement credit. Returning a same-kind key consumes its original identity
and creates another; the receipt is not object-identity preservation.

Gadof carries toy woman 59252 as G stock, although his replenishing shop list
contains other toys. The shared shop can sell positive-cost existing carried
stock, so this is not a missing purchase source. Active shop refusal is a
separate blocker. His roaming shop can act outside a fixed toyshop room.
Stock extraction on merchant death and active reset refusal mean ordinary
declaration alone does not qualify a looting or repeat-purchase route.

All 135 addressed response blocks have a valid topic alias in the thirteen
addressable contacts. The other twelve contacts explain material/access roles.
Ambient haunt, rat, bloodhulk and unsolicited action blocks are not accepted-topic
objectives. Preserve native spellings such as Exethanter's `dostran`, Rahadin's
`gaulthias` and Izek's `breech` until deliberate alias repair. Keyword replies
alone currently earn no quest achievement.

## Access, scenery, containers and exploration

The cellar entrance is Castle Ravenloft 58538 → 59057; the Kingsmen route is
58542 → 59352. Paired handprints 59326/59325 connect foreign 58456 and local
59065 through push teleports. Spectral corridor 59359 opens outward to Barovia
91164/91165; those gateways continue through 91166/91167 toward Barovia village
and Castle Ravenloft rather than being direct reciprocal catacomb entrances.
Loaded destinations do not prove a recoverable successful player journey.

Black key 59037 fits the north/south cell doors without configured break chance.
Old key 58419 is nested in keyring 58420 carried by Castle Ravenloft's Strahd
58383 in 58562. Its break chance is 100; it fits the main 59079/59082 gates and
the hidden 59351/59352 wall route. Spectral key 59288 also has break chance 100
and fits the southern 59359/59360 gate. The reverse northern gate has no key.
Kasimir's key 59257 fits Patrina's 59156/59276 crypt without fracture chance.
Strahd's crypt key 59230 is typed trash, but the shared key check uses the exact
VNUM and can accept it. It fits 59318/59320 despite being carried inside the
gaseous-form area; dimension-door and coffin routes provide alternate access.

Push wine racks 59012, pull paired curtains 59017/59018, and bleed slab 59237
for their configured passages. The slab's shared switch checks no health-fee
predicate of its own; do not invent blood-payment accounting from its prose.
Wolf statuette 59173 uses `get`, the two memories 59243/59244 use `touch`, and
the bat's dimension door 59242 uses `enter`. Carried switches require their
configured room; an operated ground switch follows different dispatch rules.
An object still inside a corpse is neither directly carried nor ground scenery.

Seventeen coffin routes and their lids expose wights and return passages.
Tomescu's misty portal reaches the Blood Rift, where some apparent portals
release enemies rather than transport the actor. Sergei's portcullis and marble
sword are pull mechanisms; the latter's strength-flavored prose is not a shared
switch strength check. Dhavit's door unlocks on the accepted last word `dhavit`
from one side or `utherlan` from the other; opening is a separate action.
Speech-door success and confirmed traversal are separate proposed evidence.

Several quest supplies are P commands inside repeated marble slabs 59045 or
mummified remains 59170. The native loader finds a matching container globally
by prototype rather than binding the P command to an exact immediately preceding
parent generation/room. Declared music-player, watch, ashes, thighbone and shroud
locations are intended placement, not verified exact-instance contents. The
journal names this limitation rather than claiming a guaranteed crypt source.

Sasha's journal 59158 is ordinary stock on her slab and gives Dayheart/Sunsword
lore. Castle Ravenloft's Dayheart 58410 is ordinary stock in 58517. The local
quest dispatcher supplies no destruction/weapon/reunion terminal for this lore;
no Sunsword object-name source was found in the active object files. This is
optional history and a builder integration lead, not a new achievement.

## Validation boundary

Focused catalog, source and native-journal regressions cover all 37 bindings,
addressable topic coverage, exact five-coin/scroll readiness, alternative artifact
credit, independent ash/skull outcomes, service exclusion, mode-zero retirement,
supplied materials, read-only projection and saved receipt recovery. Synthetic
encounters and receipts qualify journal behavior only. Active source generation,
shopping, container placement, key fracture, real travel, selected repairs and
complete player journeys remain unqualified.
