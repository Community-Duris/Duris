# The Minor Plane of Planitude

This source dossier covers the active Flind contributor: 89 rooms (#3400–#3488), 34 mobile prototypes, 41 object prototypes, and one shop record. The route, reset, dependency, title, and history review is in the [Flind audit supplement](../quest-story-followups/FLIND_AUDIT_REVIEW.md).

## A plane shaped by torture

The room text moves through a series of stone passages, water-filled chambers, a black void, and a polished golden hallway. In the Hall of Spears, thousands of sharp points cover the walls, floor, and ceiling ([room #3400](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L1)). Elsewhere, victims are chained, crucified, skinned, nailed to walls, or left for the Flind to butcher. The Skinning Room describes exposed skulls and nerve tissue ([room #3476](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L1808)); the Demon’s Walk shows bound humanoids with their brains exposed to feeding appendages ([room #3483](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L1995)).

The Butcher Shop makes the area’s cannibalism explicit. Its room description calls the displayed body parts delicacies to the Flind, and the local shop source offers an ogre finger, human feet, troll testicles, eyes, and half a rat in cheese. A smelly butcher mobile is reset there with a display case ([room #3406](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L147), [shop record](../../../areas/shp/flind.shp#L1), [resets](../../../areas/zon/flind.zon#L81)).

Water and height divide the map. A shaft opens toward the Underworld at room #3475. The nearby water cavern describes lightning-like energy around a blue portal, with a landing at #3409 ([shaft](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L1790), [water cavern](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L198), [landing](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L214)). A narrow pillar and suspended bridges hang over a vast black emptiness ([room #3431](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L703)); a later floating platform bears a gold-and-blood pentagram beside a stream of fire ([room #3462](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L1452)).

Demonic imagery ties the passages together. One burning chamber forms an inverted cross in its flames ([room #3435](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L816)). The golden corridor’s polished surface reflects vile creatures and hides a south door into a skin-covered room ([room #3487](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L2099)). Below the torture scenes, two rooms share the title Hatchery and describe living tissue and black orbs fed by anguish ([rooms #3480 and #3484](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L1929)). The final Wagering Room contains a deep pit, benches, and a platform of colorful blocks ([room #3488](../../../areas/wld/flind.wld#L2124)); the checked-in text does not explain the game’s rules or outcome.

## Inhabitants and objects

The reset roster includes Flind clerics, guards, tutors, cubs, trainers, cooks, and magi, alongside captives, rats, cavern gliders, corpse cleaners, living innards, demons, minions, and sentinels. The object set reinforces the torture, food, and demon themes with body parts, thumbscrews, symbols, weapons, and a cross-shaped key. The supplement maps the resets, doors, and six local teleport objects.

## Source boundary

The full area roster and QST roster both list Flind, but there is no local flind.qst file. The area has one local SHP record and no story sidecar or native Q/QA contract. This dossier describes checked-in source; it does not establish current reset state, shop operation, route availability, or a player’s experience.
