## A hidden valley beneath the falls

The route begins on a high plateau where twin jaguar statues guard a cave in
the mountain. Vines cling to the slick black caverns; deeper in, a small altar
with burning coals and herbs suggests an older sacred use. A waterfall pours
from the cavern into a sheltered valley. Pools and a fast river feed tropical
greenery, game trails, bamboo forest, and a flower-filled hut. Druids Anair
and Riana live apart from the jaguar-guarded court. River nymphs and merfolk
hold their own royal household beneath the falls.

The darker passages cross stone flecked with gold and lead through a secret
route to a carved throne room. Balgar's QST supplies the valley's central
history: an earlier people worshipped a jaguar king, and the jungle is now
ruled by that family. King Asunwa, Queen Sajana, Captain Irom, and the young
jaguars occupy separate clearings, a lavish cavern, and the hidden court.

## Balgar's scroll and the jade reward

Balgar first explains the jaguar statue's history. He then asks for a missing
ancient scroll from the throne-room area. The source QST requires scroll
#35315 and offers 10,000 experience plus the ancient jade plaque #35313; the
ZON places the scroll in the throne room and the plaque on Balgar. Tiny jade
key #35312 is also carried by Balgar, while jade jaguar statue #35307 is the
key for a separate locked passage. These are source-authored links; the
manifest split leaves actual quest and map availability unqualified.

The hut's shop record names Anan, mobile #35320, and stocks a jade-handled
longsword, tiger-fur boots, and jaguar-claw armor. The source has external
shop records that also use Asunwa mobile #35300, object #35301, and room
#35303. Keep each shop's numeric identity attached to its own source record.
