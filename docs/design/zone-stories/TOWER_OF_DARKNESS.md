## Actual dialogue, magic doors and travel

Quest M aliases dispatch addressed responses. The magic-door mechanism is
separate: [successful speech](../../../src/cmd/actcomm.c#L375) must pass
silence, form, underwater and speech checks before
[check_magic_doors](../../../src/cmd/actcomm.c#L186). It tests the final
keyword token with exact, case-insensitive isname. Accepted speech clears
EX_LOCKED/EX_SECRET on the selected exit and matching reciprocal exit. It
retains EX_CLOSED and records no story step. Asking every alias is not a
sequence of quest achievements; possession of a clue is not accepted speech.

- sargon: 134034 north → 134031, and 134040 east ↔ 134041 west.
- darkfather: 134049 southeast ↔ 134053 northwest. Katalia names this word.
- thothrontithos: 134073 up ↔ 134083 down. Four scroll fragments describe
  the syllables; use the actual keyword rather than inventing a new handler.
- earlion: 134073 down carries key -2 but raw exit state zero and its
  direction-triggered teleporter already reaches 134074. Do not claim a
  locked password prerequisite for this presently unlocked route.

[Teleport selection](../../../src/magic/spell_travel.c#L932) takes an actual
room ITEM_TELEPORT whose command matches directional input, before ordinary
movement. Enter uses actual inventory/equipment/room lookup. Fixed destinations
and unlimited charge -1 matter; a picture or portal name is not enough.
The illusion maze 134054–134061 contains differing direction-triggered
teleport destinations. Its center 134058 leads to 134117 through object
