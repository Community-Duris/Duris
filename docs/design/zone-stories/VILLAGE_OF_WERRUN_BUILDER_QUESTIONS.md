# Village of Werrun builder questions

Canonical key: area:werrun. Physical coverage row 344. The
[source-comprehensive dossier](VILLAGE_OF_WERRUN.md) and
[repository-wide audit](../quest-story-followups/WERRUN_AUDIT_REVIEW.md)
already document the native quest receipts, access paths, foreign consumers,
and bounded runtime uncertainties. These twenty direct questions make the
remaining content and owner decisions easy to review without changing source.

## Explicit builder questions and recommended resolutions

1. **Should Malfun’s two item exchanges remain separate story outcomes?**
   Q80 and Q91 accept different exact items and produce different rewards.
   **Recommend** keeping them as independent receipts; dialogue, reading, and
   one exchange should not imply the other.
2. **Are Malfun’s two requests intended to recur as daily content?**
   The source analysis calls them potential dailies, but reset and recipient
   renewal are not availability guarantees. **Recommend** retaining separate
   authored stories and qualifying recurrence before promising a daily.
3. **Does the braid exchange imply that Lady Death is Sklera?**
   Malfun mourns Sklera while Lady Death wears the braid, but no identity,
   transformation, or rescue state is present in the reviewed code.
   **Recommend** keeping the narrative resemblance unresolved unless the
   builder supplies an explicit endpoint.
4. **Should Malfun’s “four colors and a healing scroll” clue be corrected?**
   The reviewed desk stock has flight and venom scrolls, not green/red scrolls
   or a healing scroll. **Recommend** confirming intended stock first and
   changing descriptive copy separately from any decision to add items.
5. **Will Mage’s 350,000-copper debt exchange remain blocked pending payment
   support?**
   Q144 is coin-only, and the active quest adapter does not accept coins.
   **Recommend** leaving it blocked until a durable coin-payment path can
   settle payment and acceptance without inventing an item receipt.
6. **Is the traveller’s trap meant to be difficult to encounter?**
   The room prose calls him a rare load, while the reset chance is 100, cap 1,
   and zone mode is 2. **Recommend** preserving both facts and asking the
   builder whether the scarcity is intentional before adjusting reset behavior.
7. **Should the traveller retire after Vewon’s book is accepted?**
   Q160 has a D1 ending and the reset group is mode 2. **Recommend** retaining
   the native retirement rule while treating later availability as a separate
   reset and recipient-instance question.
8. **How should Q160’s 35,000 XP and sandals be represented for recipients?**
   The native reward has level caps and frozen recipient/recovery rules.
   **Recommend** preserving those rules and not treating quest acceptance as
   proof that every recipient received XP.
9. **Are the two Library of Doom books intended as independent found sources?**
   Revan’s and Vewon’s books have separate floor resets. **Recommend**
   documenting each exact accepted book and keeping gifts or alternate
   supplies valid without requiring personal reading or travel.
10. **Should the Revan key open only the desk stock described in the dossier?**
    White key #38308 fits desk #38307, which holds flight and venom scrolls.
    **Recommend** keeping key possession, desk access, contents, and Malfun’s
    accepted receipt as separate evidence.
11. **Is the bush at #38300 intended to open the west passage through PUSH?**
    Bush #38316 uses a typed portal to clear the blocked exit toward #38340.
    **Recommend** preserving the exact command and blocked-state condition in
    any player-facing route description.
12. **Should the broken and perfect mirrors remain a paired STARE route?**
    The mirrors at #38342 and #38344 point to each other and each consumes a
    charge. **Recommend** keeping LOOK/examination distinct from STARE travel
    and verifying settled arrivals before adding travel credit.
13. **Should crossing between the mirror-linked halves count as zone
    discovery?**
    Both destinations are in Werrun’s active zone. **Recommend** keeping this
    internal travel separate from first discovery of another zone.
14. **Are the skull key and nightmare doors intended to remain linked to the
    official?**
    Mobile #38319 carries skull key #38322, which matches doors at #38365 and
    #38366. **Recommend** preserving the exact source-key binding and reset
    flags rather than implying every encounter yields access.
15. **Does the stable offer free horses independently of the trainer’s mount?**
    Trainer #38304 is reset riding horse #38303, while stable dialogue offers
    free horses without a quest lease receipt. **Recommend** documenting the
    trainer and ordinary stable service as separate routes.
16. **Should the bartender’s generated missions count as a fifth native QST?**
    Bartender #38309 uses shared world_quest behavior, while the local QST has
    four static bindings. **Recommend** keeping generated missions separate
    from the four authored receipts unless a distinct adapter is designed.
17. **Who owns the foreign reuse of Werrun prototypes in Alatorin?**
    Five complete Alatorin reset groups use local cigar, horse, or beef
    prototypes. **Recommend** preserving Alatorin as the reset owner and Werrun
    as the prototype owner.
18. **Is Shipyards Frull’s cigar commission intended to remain a separate
    foreign quest?**
    Shipyards QST 822 consumes cigar box #38314 and pays there. **Recommend**
    keeping its acceptance and payment under Shipyards rather than adding a
    Werrun quest completion.
19. **How should Lady Death’s imported memory and rune be described?**
    The memory is accepted by a WH ambassador, and rune #358 uses a separate
    epic-stone procedure. **Recommend** keeping both foreign systems separate
    from the four local QST outcomes.
20. **Is the Nexus56 shop listing for book #38326 intended to load?**
    The unregistered nexus56.shp product and reset exist, but keeper binding
    and player access are unqualified; this checkout lacks generated
    areas/world.shp and its shop loader has a source-file fallback.
    **Recommend** asking the shop owner to confirm the listing before treating
    it as available or removing the source evidence.
