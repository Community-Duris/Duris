mobile prototypes, and 11 object prototypes. Its active title is “The
Underworld,” also used by the separate `underworld` contributor; the shared
title does not establish a merge or replacement. The full-world and quest area
indexes both include this stem, while the current source inventory lists no
local QST, SHP, or story sidecar. See the [full-world index](../../../areas/AREA#L145),

lake, rivers, a swamp, the crumbling Tower of Kromor, and several external
boundaries. Mob and object descriptions add myconids, slime pools, ropers,
elementals, aquatic creatures, miners, fishers, a warning sign, and paired
shimmering portals. The reset file places these authored prototypes and
resets a closed boulder pair. Room #30162 connects to the Tower of Kromor in
Mistywood; the existing [Mistywood review](MISTYWOOD.md#access-hazards-and-unresolved-stories)
covers that adjoining area's tower and Kromor story. These static records
document authored material; they do not by themselves establish current
loading, actor access, safe travel, live stock, or a quest contract.
