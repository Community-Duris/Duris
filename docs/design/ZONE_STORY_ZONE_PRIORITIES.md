# Zone journal integration priorities

**Reviewed planning order — October 4, 2026. Active economic accounting is required.**

This is an integration order, not a difficulty ranking or a claim that players must follow
these stories in order. New journals use the existing schema; proposed learned dialogue,
personal recovery, kill credit, scripted access/travel, branches, and full-stage families
remain explicitly untracked until their semantic adapters and persistence are qualified.

Priority weighs newcomer value, coherent progression, reuse of universal capabilities,
size of a qualifying player journey, and unresolved accounting/world dependencies.
Raw contract count alone is not a priority score. The [complete active inventory](../reference/ZONE_STORY_ZONE_INVENTORY.md)
covers every catalog zone, including Q-free areas with dialogue or assigned special leads.

## Deferred and Q-free content

- `heavens` is administrative content; its nine Q contracts do not become player stories/dailies.
- `vehicles` has 255 Q contracts, predominantly equipment/service-style exchanges; classify
  actual narrative outcomes before promoting them to deeper stories. High volume is not proof of quest value.
- Q-free zones are all retained in the inventory. The Plains of Life is deliberately high
  priority because its tutorial special has a concrete accepted action and travel gate.
  Other Q-free special assignments are inspection leads, not automatically quests.
- Ordinary bartender/random world quests remain outside this stable zone-story catalog.

## Qualification checklist per story family

1. Record source area, actual giver ownership, canonical native bindings, mapped revision,
   source locations, and reviewed/unreviewed branches.
2. Verify executable items/counts/fees/rewards and the actual source/access predicates.
   Distinguish possession from personal history and guidance from mandatory conditions.
3. Qualify discovery, actual visible encounters, reveals, next action, alternatives,
   gifts, groups, missing prerequisites, failed exchanges, and a cold restart.
4. For new semantic stages, prove accepted-event timing, deduplication, storage failure,
   attempt boundaries, transformation lineage, and recovery without new rewards.
5. Record coverage separately for static bindings, dialogue, scripts, provenance,
   economics, client presentation, and the actual played journey; update this queue.
