# Zone journal integration priorities

**Reviewed planning order — October 2, 2026. Active economic accounting is required.**

This is an integration order, not a difficulty ranking or a claim that players must follow
these stories in order. New journals use the existing schema; proposed learned dialogue,
personal recovery, kill credit, scripted access/travel, branches, and full-stage families
remain explicitly untracked until their semantic adapters and persistence are qualified.

Priority weighs newcomer value, coherent progression, reuse of universal capabilities,
size of a qualifying player journey, and unresolved accounting/world dependencies.
Raw contract count alone is not a priority score. The [complete active inventory](../reference/ZONE_STORY_ZONE_INVENTORY.md)
covers every catalog zone, including Q-free areas with dialogue or assigned special leads.

Follow the [execution register](ZONE_STORY_ROADMAP_EXECUTION.md) for completed
source dossiers and precise blockers. Priorities 1 through 16 now have comprehensive
source maps; the other entries retain their earlier rough/provisional status.
Active reset-generation qualification is a shared prerequisite for the pilots.

## New authored journal batch

Eight sidecars were added in this pass: `breale`, `elvish`, `krimman`, `bastine`,
`pineholl`, `quietus`, `torg`, and `solonar`. All 86 owning native Q contracts
are classified. Preparation services remain visible after their giver is encountered,
without adding story/daily achievements. Native receipts and rewards remain unchanged.

Preparation is described as one valid route, never a required personal acquisition.
Schema 3 supports optional checks against live supplies and existing receipts.
It does not yet express new historical event kinds, conditional subrecipes,
all-stage campaign completion, mutually exclusive choices, or stage-by-stage spoiler
reveal. Consequently, independent Bastine promotions and Triad exchanges stay distinct.
An any-of `contracts` array is used only for actual alternatives, such as Quietus
credentials or Torg's equivalent fine-chisel makers.

Returned items need semantic review: Krimeneha's staff exchanges genuinely release
spirits; Quietus credentials provide briefings; chrysloin wrong-color returns are
rejections. The mere presence of a returned item cannot decide the classification.

Breale's hats, brooms and wands are riddle terms explained by east/west
Passage drawings, with dirt, fennel, peppercorn, wood, mushroom and ash as the
accepted reagents. Preserve that puzzle. Separately, its learned-fire-spell
prose differs from actual potion/amulet rewards and needs a builder decision.
Bastine also describes rank and kill progress without enforcing those facts;
receiving an item is not kill proof. The Homestead shows why key targets,
spoken magic-door properties and consumable potion recipes need review even
when no local special is assigned. Its source dossier accounts for those
routes; accepted travel, learned lore and personal source history remain open.

Krimeneha's reviewed route distinguishes fragment preparation, seven real
rescues and the lord's independent keepsake finale. Eckraldu can roam into
other areas, and Quietus also consumes his staff. Bastine's narrated order
is now reflected in its journal, while source hints identify all twelve
accepted trophies. Victor's continuation uses four ordinary key targets and
Morlanthra's own Highway receipt. Replacement item identity, complete household/
rank campaigns and owned cross-zone projection are explicit shared-plan additions.

Pine Hollow's complete source review adds the competing faerie equipment route,
roaming proof carriers, actual mine/smelter keys and shared traps, and the marble
eye's Skelenak continuation. The coat's two-skin demand conflicts with its only
producer's ordinary live cap of one. A one-way holding exit can also affect proof
availability. Both concerns have concrete reproduction and builder repair plans;
seven independent local achievements and all native terms remain unchanged.

Quietus's comprehensive source review now explains all four missions, ten
credential alternatives, both captain proof services and all native topic
families. Optional briefings preserve supplied-proof routes. Its rare load
rooms are intentional availability mechanics needing qualification; badge
replacement can consume nested contents. Bartender world quests retain their
separate namespace. Shared world-quest refunds and ship coin settlement gaps
are recorded with targeted repair plans that preserve existing committed
reward/epic adapters and the inn's terminal-save behavior.

Torg's comprehensive source review explains all fifteen item-only contracts and
all fifteen native topic families. Optional hide/rose/locket preparation preserves
supplied materials. Two promise rings and two chisel kinds remain distinct; the
eight legends need one of each relic, while Tranug's three drops are independent.
Recipients arriving with the invasion require confirmed movement, and live
cap-one sources can wander into an exitless holding room. The legend scroll
produces one random foreign tale half; Lanella produces one random heart for
Sierra's owned Winterhaven contracts. Direct birth/transform ordering, absent
timer consumption, unattached forge dispatch and disabled altar content have
concrete repair or builder-decision plans without invented local completion.

## Deferred and Q-free content

- `heavens` is administrative content; its nine Q contracts do not become player stories/dailies.
- `vehicles` has 255 Q contracts, predominantly equipment/service-style exchanges; classify
  actual narrative outcomes before promoting them to deeper stories. High volume is not proof of quest value.
- Q-free zones are all retained in the inventory. The Plains of Life is deliberately high
  priority because its tutorial special has a concrete accepted action and travel gate.
  Other Q-free special assignments are inspection leads, not automatically quests.
- Ordinary bartender/random world quests remain outside this stable zone-story catalog.

## Qualification checklist per story family

1. Record source area, actual giver ownership, canonical native bindings, mapped revision,
   source locations, and reviewed/unreviewed branches.
2. Verify executable items/counts/fees/rewards and the actual source/access predicates.
   Distinguish possession from personal history and guidance from mandatory conditions.
3. Qualify discovery, actual visible encounters, reveals, next action, alternatives,
   gifts, groups, missing prerequisites, failed exchanges, and a cold restart.
4. For new semantic stages, prove accepted-event timing, deduplication, storage failure,
   attempt boundaries, transformation lineage, and recovery without new rewards.
5. Record coverage separately for static bindings, dialogue, scripts, provenance,
   economics, client presentation, and the actual played journey; update this queue.
