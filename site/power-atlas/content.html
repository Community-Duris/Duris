Findings

What the numbers say

  • Class decides; race multiplies.Class explains 79–91% of the spread in one-on-one strength at every level, race 11–27%. Within a class, race scales the result rather than reordering the classes.
  • One-on-one, casters out-damage melee from level 11.At level 56 in end-game gear a warrior lands about 2 damage per second on a geared player. A sorcerer's best spell does 46, an ethermancer's 59. Melee is cut three ways: the global −75%, armour that keeps reducing damage down to −750, and about half of all swings parried, blocked or dodged.
  • Big bodies win.Troll, Ogre and Firbolg run 2–6× their class average from level 11 on, almost entirely through hit points, which grow with the square of racial Constitution. Humans sit at or near the bottom of every class from level 31 (0.60–0.76×).
  • The gaps open at three levels.Level 11 brings the first real attack spells, and the spread between classes goes from 23× to 123×. Level 21 brings psionicist inflict pain and druid earthquake. At level 51 end-game gear arrives, and stacked globe and ward items block most circle 1–7 arcane spells.
  • Pulse, shrug and innates matter less.Pulse moves a combination by about ±10%, mostly helping small-race fighters. Shrug matters mainly at end-game, where it lifts the shrugging races most. The only large innate is troll regeneration.
  • The halfling warrior is weak, but not the weakest.It ranks 13th to 19th of 21 warriors. At level 56 its hit points hold it back (×0.65) and its size (×0.87), partly offset by faster pulse (×1.17). Two warrior races rank below it. The warrior class as a whole has the bigger problem in the late game.
  • Specialisation can matter more than race.From level 31 every build is in the field and each race and class is scored with its best one. Ethermancer edges Shaman at level 56 end-game (7.5 against 7.1). Healer clerics and end-game conjurers who stay unspecialised are the weakest builds of all, and 22 specialisations are never the best choice for any race, level or gear.

Read the index as an order, not a margin. Every duel is one continuous fight on full memorisation, with no fleeing and no re-memorising, so running out of spell slots decides many caster fights and the gaps (×100 and more) overstate real play. Ranking by plain share of duels won gives almost the same order (rank correlation 0.95–0.99). Openers such as backstab are left out of the duel, so rogues are understated.

Tier levels

Where the gaps open

Spell circles open at levels 1, 6, 11 … 56 (circle N at 5N − 4). The steps below compare each report level with the one before, on the usual gear. A combination is flagged when it moves 15 percentile points or ×1.42 in index.

Step What changes
1 → 6 First attack spells. Minotaur sorcerer ×4.2 (burning hands, chill touch); druids fall.
6 → 11 Casters overtake melee. Shamans get pythonsting and scalding blast (ogre and firbolg shamans ×12); summoners get shocking grasp. The class spread goes from 24× to 120×.
16 → 21 Psionicist inflict pain (gith psionicists ×37); druid earthquake (×9–11).
21 → 26 Earthen maul for druids (×3.3); necromancer cone of cold, enervation and minor globe (×3).
26 → 31 Specialisations at 30: flame reaver lifts reavers ×4.5.
31 → 36 Basic to good gear. Melee gains (drow and duergar rogues ×2.3).
50 → 51 End-game gear, the largest shift. Stacked globe and spirit-ward items block most circle 1–7 arcane spells: summoners ×0.10, conjurers ×0.37; ethermancer 2.5 → 6.4, blighter 2.0 → 4.7, druid 2.5 → 5.0.
51 → 56 Conjurers recover ×2.6–3.2 as more unblocked high-circle slots arrive.

Racial factors

What makes a race strong

Each racial term was switched off in turn, with gear held fixed, to see how far it moves a combination's index. Hit points are the dominant racial factor. Pulse, shrug and innates are real but smaller, apart from troll regeneration.

Case study

The halfling warrior

The halfling warrior is below average among warriors at most levels, and the larger problem is the warrior class itself, which trails most classes in the late game. On good gear it climbs to 13th of 21 as a Guardian; at level 56 its best build is Swordsman. The table follows its best build at each level.

Why, at level

End-game

End-game turns on globe gear

Every end-game kit the model builds stacks permanent globe and ward items, which zero the matching spells (fight.c:4562–4584), almost every circle 1–7 arcane attack. Removing just those four gear effects, with the same items and specialisations, reverses much of the end-game order. How easy those items are to get is the biggest end-game balance lever in the model.

Reality check

Against real PvP picks

The public PvP log records 2,210 distinct level-50+ characters since 2017. Pick rates are normalised so 1 means a race is picked as often as its side and number of legal classes would predict. The model agrees with players most on races and weakly on classes: players over-pick psionicists and necromancers and under-pick shamans and ethermancers, which the model rates highest. Pick rates also reflect group roles, history and fashion, so this is a sanity check, not proof.

Method and limits

How this was built

  • Source. Races, classes, skills, spells, melee and spell formulas were transcribed from master f3b66b07f, which includes the revised racial pulse (#333). The original report references file and line citations in separate working files; those files are not included in this snapshot.
  • Reported engine check. The original report states that a harness compiled all 432 server files, loaded the live properties and enumerated every random outcome. Against it, the model's round length, cast time, HP, stats, to-hit and melee damage showed 0 deviations over 1% in about 37,000 checks. Spell resists, shrug and saves are exact. 16 of 1,334 spell-damage cases fell outside sampling tolerance, each explained.
  • Gear. Three kits were built per combination from the 10,424 wearable items in booted zones, within the engine's caps: gear HP ≤ 3 × level, hitroll ≤ 75, damroll ≤ 50 × the race multiplier, and stats ≤ 100 plus racial-maximum gear. Basic uses typical level 20–35 items, good uses upper-quartile level 40–50 items, and end-game uses the best level 55+ items. Class and race restrictions were respected.
  • Duel. Time-to-kill both ways from melee, spells, pets, control and healing, with skills at the maximum for each level, full spell slots. Below level 30 the field is the 192 race and class combinations; from level 31 it is all 711 builds (every specialisation plus unspecialised), each counted once, and each race and class is scored with its best build. The index is capped at 1/100–100 per matchup.
  • Not modelled. Openers (backstab and the like appear only as a separate burst figure), bard songs, reaver weapon procs, spell disables inside the duel, groups, fleeing and re-memorising, and randomly generated gear.
  • Engine findings along the way. Normal melee and all spell damage are multiplied by 0.25. Sanctuary only works against melee for clerics and paladins. The spell-penetration cap is 60, because its properties key is misspelt. Thri-Kreen and Barbarian levelling factors fall back to 1.0 for the same kind of reason. Minotaur charge can't hit Medium or Small races. Duergar battlerage haste lasts longer than its cooldown.
